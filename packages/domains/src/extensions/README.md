# The extensions: `extensions`

The kernel's office of the extensions (EXT-7, EXT-8): the place where the node's user's choices about the extensions are kept, which extension she activated, with which manifest accepted, and to which bonds she withdrew the offer; and the questions her node asks a bond about the extensions in common. It knows an extension by its manifest only, in its own words (GR-3: the kernel's adapter translates the boundary's manifest into it, GR-10), and a bond by the address book's identity. Its rules are EXT-7, EXT-8 and EXT-10 ("What asks more") of [`architecture.md`](../../../../docs/architecture.md), with GR-3, GR-4, PER-9, DOM-6, F7 and F8; its words in the [glossary](../../../../docs/glossary.md) (installed, activate, deactivate, offered to a bond, withdraw the offer, extensions in common, the activations).

- [`activations/`](activations/), the office (`attivazioni`): the user's commands on `self`, never a peer's (EXT-7), `activate_an_extension` (the manifest shown and accepted, which must be the one installed), `deactivate_an_extension`, `withdraw_the_offer_from_a_bond` and `offer_an_extension_to_a_bond` (the bond one of hers, read over the address book's state in the same step, GR-3); `ask_a_bond_for_the_extensions_in_common`, with its effect `ask_for_the_extensions_in_common` (`order: 'any'`) and its return `learn_the_extensions_in_common`; the reads `an_extension` (the manifest installed and whether it is active, not active, or waiting for the new manifest), `the_extensions_offered_to_a_bond` and `the_extensions_in_common` (the server's side of EXT-8). What an update asks more is `dsl.what_it_asks_more`. The manifests installed are the composition's, which the state adapter is given as data.

## Stories to prove

Each story is the test named after it, in `packages/memory/test/extensions/activations/activations.test.ts`, on q-'s oracle (the module `oracle/extensions.ts`, on the model of `bonds.ts`: a phone's cell with the office's state beside the address book's, MBX-7), entering the office's use cases as the user's entry would (`self`, step 13), the node's clock and the operations injected (TST-2, TST-3). The manifest installed is the test extension's, greetings (`saluti`), composed from its `manifest.json` and translated by the kernel's adapter (`the_manifest_to_accept`); an update of the app is the same cell with another manifest installed. The bonds the stories start from are kept in the address book, as its stories make them (part 11.2). A question's delivery is the story's: the kernel's port of requests and its leaf on `known` are step 13's.

The names: Anna's phone, her face F_a, her bonds L_b with Bruno and L_c with Carla; Bruno's phone, his face F_b, his bond L_a with Anna. Friday 20 November 2026, 10:00 in Rome. Greetings 0.1.0 (its leaf `greet` on known faces, requests on her bonds and its storage, an action on a bond, majors 1 on `self` and on `known`), preinstalled.

1. **Off** (`a preinstalled extension is off until the user activates it`). `an_extension` for greetings gives its manifest as installed, not active; it is offered to no bond; an extension the node does not have has no entry.
2. **Activated** (`activated with the manifest shown, it is active and offered to every bond`). Anna activates greetings with the manifest she is shown: `an_extension_has_been_activated`, which records the manifest accepted and the instant; it is active, and offered to L_b and L_c, at major 1. Activated again: `an_extension_has_been_activated_again`.
3. **Not the one installed** (`a manifest other than the installed one is refused`). A manifest of another version, or of an extension the node does not have: refused, `the_extension_is_installed_with_this_manifest` failed, no fact; greetings stays off.
4. **Deactivated** (`deactivated, it is not active and offered to no bond`). Deactivating greetings before she activated it: `an_extension_has_been_deactivated_again`. Activated, then deactivated at 11:00: `an_extension_has_been_deactivated`; not active, offered to no bond; again, `again`.
5. **An update that asks more** (`an update that asks more waits for the new manifest until accepted`). Activated at 0.1.0, the app updated with greetings 0.2.0, which also sees the bond's name: it waits for the new manifest, what it asks more `to_see_on_known` `the_name_of_the_bond`, offered to no bond; the old manifest no longer accepted; the new one accepted, active and offered again.
6. **An update that asks no more** (`an update that asks no more keeps it active`). Greetings 0.2.0 with a second major on both personas, the same leaves: active, offered at majors 1 and 2. One with a new leaf on `known` waits, `a_leaf_on_known`.
7. **Withdrawn** (`the offer withdrawn from one bond, still offered to the others`). Anna withdraws the offer of greetings from L_c: `the_offer_of_an_extension_has_been_withdrawn_from_a_bond`; offered to L_b, not to L_c; still active. Again: `again`.
8. **Offered again** (`offered again after a withdrawal, a change; offered already, again`). Offered to L_c again: `an_extension_has_been_offered_to_a_bond`, a change, so no `again`; offered to L_b, where it was: `an_extension_has_been_offered_to_a_bond_again`.
9. **A bond not hers** (`withdrawing from a bond that does not exist is refused`). Withdrawing from L_x, never a bond of hers, or from L_c once she removed it (BND-6), and offering to L_c then: refused, `the_bond_exists` failed. Withdrawing the offer of an extension the node does not have: refused, `the_extension_is_installed`.
10. **Across a deactivation** (`a withdrawal stays across a deactivation`). Withdrawn from L_c, deactivated, activated again: still offered to L_b only. Her choice stays as she left it.
11. **The extensions in common, served** (`the extensions in common: those offered back, with their majors`). Bruno's phone, greetings active, read for L_a with what Anna's says it offers (greetings at major 1): greetings, at major 1.
12. **Served empty** (`the extensions in common: empty after the offer is withdrawn, or the extension deactivated`). Bruno withdraws the offer from L_a: empty; offered again and deactivated: empty.
13. **A claim of more** (`the extensions in common: a claim of more learns no more`). Anna's list claims ping pong, greetings at major 7 and waves: Bruno's answers greetings at major 1, what a request of greetings would learn, and nothing of the others (F2, F8). An empty list: empty.
14. **Asked, learned** (`the asker’s question and what came back, recorded`). Anna's phone asks on L_b with what it offers him, as `the_extensions_offered_to_a_bond` reads it: `a_bond_has_been_asked_for_the_extensions_in_common`, the question waiting; started, the executor delivers `ask_for_the_extensions_in_common` (the bond, greetings at major 1, 60 seconds), the story's delivery answering from Bruno's read; `the_extensions_in_common_with_a_bond_have_been_learned`, greetings at major 1. Bruno withdraws the offer from Anna: the next question learns nothing in common.
15. **No answer** (`no response: the question had no answer`). Nothing comes back in its time: `a_question_about_the_extensions_in_common_had_no_answer`, `no_response`; no dead letter.
16. **What it tells** (`a question tells only what the node offers the bond`). Telling greetings while it is off, on L_c after the offer was withdrawn from it, or at majors it does not serve: refused, `these_extensions_are_offered_to_the_bond`; on a bond not hers, `the_bond_exists`. Telling nothing is a question all the same.

Beside them: `what the kernel reads of the office` (same file), the kernel's adapter over the office's read ports (`the_kernels_reads` in `adapters-common`: `is_active`, `is_offered`, the lists `route` takes); and `packages/adapters-common/test/extensions/activations.test.ts`, the translation of a composed manifest (`the_manifest_to_accept`) and what an update asks more over two translated manifests.
