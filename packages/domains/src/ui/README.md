# The screens: `ui`

The screen model of the phone (V2, ADP-7, D76): the screens of stage 1, their presentational state, what happens on them and what they show, as a pure domain of values and functions; the Solid rendering is its adapter (step 22). It sees of `user` its values and identities (`sight.ts`, `ui: { user: ['value', 'id'] }`, GR-2): what a gesture asks of her node is a value of `user`'s, which the rendering hands to `user`'s use cases; `ui` invokes nothing. Its rules are UI-1..UI-5 of [`architecture.md`](../../../../docs/architecture.md), with V2, ADP-7, GR-2, EXT-7, EXT-16, USR-2..USR-8, WEB-1, WEB-2, WEB-5; its words in the [glossary](../../../../docs/glossary.md).

- [`value.ts`](value.ts), [`dsl.ts`](dsl.ts): the words shared by the screens: a screen, the way (the screens she opened), where a gesture goes, what it asks, the course of what a screen asked, the places and the kernel's mark of an extension (EXT-16), and what the screens are given to show, in their words; where the app starts, the way after a gesture, the marks a place holds.
- One folder per screen, each with its state, what happens on it, a turn (its state after, what it asks, where it goes), what it is given and what it shows; and `at_the_start`, `after`, `what_it_shows`: [`the_first_face/`](the_first_face/), [`my_contacts/`](my_contacts/), [`a_contact/`](a_contact/), [`the_handshake_in_person/`](the_handshake_in_person/), [`the_handshake_at_a_distance/`](the_handshake_at_a_distance/), [`an_invitation/`](an_invitation/), [`the_extensions/`](the_extensions/), [`an_extension/`](an_extension/), [`a_page/`](a_page/), [`what_happened/`](what_happened/), [`the_server/`](the_server/).
