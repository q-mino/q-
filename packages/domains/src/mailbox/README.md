# The relay: `mailbox` and `desk`

The relay is a post office with no clerk who reads: it keeps the envelopes left on a deposit address D for seven days, and hands them to whoever shows the collect secret R of that mailbox (D = hash(R)). It knows no sender and opens no envelope (F2). Its rules are MBX-1..MBX-4 of [`architecture.md`](../../../../docs/architecture.md), with PER-19, F5, DOM-17, V5; its words in the [glossary](../../../../docs/glossary.md).

- [`deposits/`](deposits/), the relay's business: `deposit_an_envelope`, kept with the time the relay received it, by its clock (MBX-1); the wait of seven days from the deposit, whose return `discard_an_envelope` throws the envelope away, collected or not (MBX-2); `the_envelopes_in_a_mailbox`, read and collected as a stream from a position, a collect taking nothing away (MBX-3).
- [`../desk/`](../desk/), its personas (PER-19): the node `desk` takes the `Caller` as the interface verified it, nothing or a collect secret turned into its D; `depositor` (anyone) deposits; `holder` (who shows a collect secret) collects the mailbox it names, refused unless the secret is that mailbox's.

## Stories to prove

Each story is the test named after it (part 8.2), in [`packages/memory/test/relay/stories.ts`](../../../memory/test/relay/stories.ts), on q-'s oracle (MBX-7: the harness `harness.ts`, on the model of Shed's `scenarios/oracle.ts`), through the desk's trees, as its interface enters them (MBX-6: a depositor who shows nothing, a holder who shows R, hashed into D), the relay's clock and the operations injected (TST-2, TST-3). The stories are a suite parametrized by the relay's factory (TST-1), run on the oracle streamed natively and composed by the library; an adapter of `mailbox` runs them as its conformance suite: the relay's storage on SQLite, through the relay's configurator ([`on_sqlite.test.ts`](../../../memory/test/relay/on_sqlite.test.ts), part 19, MBX-8), its collect read again from a position. The names: Anna's phone deposits on Bruno's mailbox (D_b, his R_b) and Carla's (D_c, R_c); E1, E2, E3 are sealed envelopes.

1. **A deposit is kept, and collected** (`a deposit is kept and its holder collects it`). Monday 9:02, relay's clock: Anna's phone deposits E1 on D_b and reads `the_envelope_is_kept` with `deposited_at` 9:02. Bruno's phone, with R_b, collects D_b: the snapshot holds E1 (its deposit id, 9:02, the envelope as deposited) and a position P1.
2. **A retry is the same deposit** (`a deposit sent again is kept once`). Anna's phone has no answer and sends the deposit of E1 again, under the same operation: it reads `the_envelope_is_kept`, 9:02, from the recorded fact (DOM-11); Bruno collects one envelope. Under another operation, E1 again is a second deposit: the relay does not compare envelopes.
3. **A collect takes nothing away** (`a collect leaves the envelopes in the mailbox`). Bruno collects D_b twice: E1 both times; no fact is recorded by a collect.
4. **A collect resumes from a position** (`a collect resumes from the position it left off`). Bruno collected up to P1. Anna deposits E2, then E3. Bruno collects from P1: the stream gives E1 again at P1 (a resume is inclusive, DOM-17), then E2 at P2 and E3 at P3; his phone drops E1 by its position and identity, and keeps P3. The positions come in the order of the deposits, and his phone compares them only by order (DOM-17 [Q]). On a stream the library composes over the log, a collect from a position cannot resume: it is `position_unavailable`, and Bruno's phone reads again, as in story 9 (DOM-18, MBX-7).
5. **A collect is live** (`a live collect gets each new envelope`). Bruno's phone follows D_b past `caught_up`: when Anna deposits E2, the stream gives it with its entry and position.
6. **Seven days, then discarded** (`an envelope is discarded seven days after its deposit`). E1 deposited Monday 9:02 is in the mailbox the next Monday at 9:01; at 9:02, by the relay's clock, the wait's return discards it (`an_envelope_has_been_discarded`): Bruno's live collect reads `left` for E1, and a collect from then on does not hold it. The same for E2, which Bruno never collected (`an envelope never collected is discarded too`). A redelivery of the wait is the duplicate branch: one discard (`the wait delivered again discards once`).
7. **Only with R** (`a mailbox is collected only with its collect secret`). Carla's phone, with R_c, collects D_b: refused, `the_collect_secret_is_the_mailboxs`, and no envelope of Bruno's. A phone that shows no secret is not found by `holder` (`no collect secret, no holder`); it may still deposit, as `depositor`, on any D (`anyone deposits, with no secret`).
8. **Each mailbox its own** (`each mailbox holds only its deposits`). Anna deposits E1 on D_b and E2 on D_c: Bruno collects E1 alone, Carla E2 alone.
9. **A position no longer kept** (`a collect from a position no longer kept reads again`). The stream from a position the adapter no longer keeps gives `position_unavailable` (TST-4); Bruno's phone collects again with no position: the snapshot, then the stream.
10. **What the relay does not take** (`an envelope too large or not base64url is not deposited`). An envelope over 87 382 characters (64 KiB decoded), or with a character out of base64url, is `invalid_request` before any node; a deposit address out of its form, too. Nothing is recorded.
