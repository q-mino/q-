# The personas: `persona`

The kernel's personas (PER-1..PER-7, EXT-1): the trees a request enters, by who asks. The base node at the top takes the `Caller` the kernel's entry gives (PER-4): **the user** of the node, from the app; **a face**, by the key a letter's signature holds for, to the mailbox of mine it came to, both verified by the post; or **anonymous**, a letter unsigned or whose signature does not hold (PER-2), with its annotation. Each persona's node verifies its own (PER-7); its leaves invoke the use cases of `bonds` and `extensions` in the persona's own words (D45, GR-3), and the business gets the persona's values as input, never the peer's (PER-8, D33). Its rules are PER-1..PER-14 and EXT-1, EXT-6, EXT-8, EXT-11 of [`architecture.md`](../../../../docs/architecture.md), with F2, F6, F7 and F8; its words in the [glossary](../../../../docs/glossary.md) (§ Personas of q-; the user, a face, anonymous, introduce myself).

- [`node.ts`](node.ts), the base (`persona`): it takes the `Caller` and verifies no hat. Its values: the `Caller`, an ADT by `is`, with the face's key and the mailbox in the post's formats, declared again (the persona sees auth's lookups and identities, not its values); its functions: `a_face_at_my_mailbox`, the key of auth's lookups, and `the_caller_is_the_user`.
- [`self/`](self/) (`io`): found only for the user (PER-3), it gives nothing more. Its leaves, the user's gestures from the app: the address book's (`make_my_first_face`, `prepare_a_ticket`, `open_their_ticket`, `rename_a_bond`, `remove_a_bond`; the reads `my_faces`, `my_bonds`, `a_bond`, `my_ticket`) and the office of the extensions' (`activate_an_extension`, `deactivate_an_extension`, `withdraw_the_offer_from_a_bond`, `offer_an_extension_to_a_bond`, `ask_a_bond_for_the_extensions_in_common`; the reads `an_extension`, `the_extensions_offered_to_a_bond`). Not what only a reaction, a return or a delivery invokes (PER-12), nor the reads the post and auth take through their ports.
- [`known/`](known/) (`volto conosciuto`): its node looks up the bond of the face at my mailbox (`the_bond_of_a_face_at_my_mailbox`) and gives it whole (`context.bond`), or closes `not_found`. Its kernel's leaf `the_extensions_in_common` (EXT-8), the bond the node's, never the asker's word; the leaves of the active extensions are mounted here at wiring (EXT-11).
- [`unknown/`](unknown/) (`volto sconosciuto`): its node looks up the ticket the face presents at my mailbox (`the_ticket_a_face_presents_at_my_mailbox`) and gives it whole (`context.ticket`), or closes `not_found`. Its leaf `introduce_myself` (PER-11), over `accept_their_introduction`: the bond the ticket's, their face's key the signer's (PER-8).
- [`anonymous/`](anonymous/) (`anonimo`): no node, anyone wears it; no leaf, its version only: every letter that wears it is `invalid_request` (`not_a_leaf`) and traced (F6, PER-14).

## Stories to prove

Each story is the test named after it, in `packages/memory/test/persona/personas.test.ts` (part 13.3), on q-'s oracle: a phone's cell with the address book and the office of the extensions, as their stories make them (MBX-7), the kernel's entry of part 13.2 building the `Caller` from the letter the post opened, or from the app; the node's clock and the operations injected (TST-2, TST-3). Every closing is checked in the tree's trace with why (F6).

The names, as the address book's and the office's stories give them: Anna's phone, her face F_a "Anna"; her bond L_b with Bruno, his key K_b, his mailbox D_b, hers for him D_mb; her bond L_c with Carla, K_c, D_c, hers for her D_mc; her ticket L_d, ready at D_md, sent to Dario in a link; Dario's key K_d, his mailbox D_d. Bruno's phone, his bond L_a with Anna. Greetings preinstalled and active on both phones, offered to every bond. Friday 20 November 2026, 10:00 in Rome.

1. **The user on `self`** (`self is worn by the user only`). Anna on her phone reads `my_bonds` on `self`: "Bruno" and "Carla", with F_a, their faces and the confirmations, in her words. Bruno's letter at D_mb invoking `my_bonds` on `self`: `not_found`, no reason, traced; an unsigned letter likewise.
2. **Her gestures** (`the user's gestures on self are told in her words`). Anna renames L_b "Bru": `the_bond_is_renamed`; removes L_c: `the_bond_is_removed`, again `the_bond_was_removed_already`, renamed after `there_is_no_such_bond`. She withdraws the offer of greetings from L_b: `the_offer_is_withdrawn`; offers it again: `the_extension_is_offered_again`. Each the business's fact, projected.
3. **Bruno on `known`** (`a face's letter on its bond reaches known with the bond whole`). Bruno's letter, signed K_b, at D_mb, invoking `the_extensions_in_common`: the node finds L_b and gives it whole, L_b, F_a, K_b, "Bruno", to the leaf.
4. **A known key outside its bond** (`a known key at the mailbox of another bond is not found, traced`). Bruno's letter, signed K_b, at D_mc, the mailbox of Anna's bond with Carla: `not_found`, no reason; the trace says the bond of the face was not known there. At D_mc once Carla's bond is removed, Carla's letter likewise.
5. **The extensions in common** (`the extensions in common on known: the bond from the node, never the input`). On Bruno's phone Anna's letter on L_a, with greetings at major 1: greetings at major 1, read for L_a, the bond the node gave. Bruno withdraws the offer from L_a: empty. An input that names a bond, or a field beyond `offered`: `invalid_request` before any node (a strict schema).
6. **Dario introduces himself** (`an introduction at a ready ticket makes the bond, with the signer's key`). Dario's letter, signed K_d, at D_md, invoking `introduce_myself` with L_d's secret, "Dario" and D_d on the relay: `you_are_bonded`; the address book records `a_bond_has_been_made_by_their_introduction` on L_d, their face K_d, the signer's, never an input's, named "Dario", the confirmation received. His next letter at D_md is `known`.
7. **The crossed QR** (`an introduction at the mailbox of a bond kept with that face confirms it`). Anna opened Bruno's ticket presenting L_b's at 10:00:05: L_b kept at D_mb, the confirmation awaited. His introduction at D_mb at 10:00:10, signed K_b, on `unknown`: `you_met_in_person`; at 10:03: `the_bond_is_confirmed`; again: `you_introduced_yourself_already`.
8. **No ticket there** (`an introduction at a mailbox with no ticket is not found`). Dario's introduction at D_b, a mailbox not Anna's; at the mailbox of a ticket expired, or of one Carla used (a ticket used twice); at D_mb, a bond kept with another face: `not_found`, no reason, traced; no fact.
9. **A ticket that cannot be used** (`an introduction refused by the address book is told so, traced`). Dario's introduction at D_md with a secret not L_d's (another ticket's, or signed by another face): `the_ticket_cannot_be_used`, no fact; the trace names the predicate that failed, `the_secret_is_the_ticket_s` (F6).
10. **Unsigned, or a signature that does not hold** (`an unsigned letter, or one whose signature does not hold, is anonymous`). An unsigned letter at D_mb, and one whose signature does not hold claiming K_b, each invoking a leaf of `known`, of `unknown` and of `anonymous`: `not_found` on `known` and `unknown` (no face, no lookup runs), `invalid_request` (`not_a_leaf`) on `anonymous`; each traced with its annotation (`none`; `does_not_hold`, claimed K_b); never `known` as Bruno (PER-2).
