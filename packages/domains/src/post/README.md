# The protocol: `post`

The post of the node: it puts a letter in an envelope and opens one, tells who sent it, and keeps the record of the requests the port of requests sends, until their response or their time. Its cryptography is the safe's, through the post's machine (GR-5). Its rules are PST-1..PST-14 of [`architecture.md`](../../../../docs/architecture.md), with F2..F5, F7, F8, GR-5, GR-6, PER-2, PER-4, PER-13, DOM-11, DOM-13..DOM-15, EXT-2, EXT-12, V18, V19 and G4; its words in the [glossary](../../../../docs/glossary.md).

- [`value.ts`](value.ts), [`dsl.ts`](dsl.ts), the protocol: the letter (a request or a response) and its signed content, which covers D, at most 32 KiB of content (PST-12); the envelope; the identities of F7 (`the_identity_of_a_request`, `the_identity_of_the_response_to`, `the_id_of_an_anonymous_letter`); `a_letter_in_an_envelope` and `the_letter_in_an_envelope`, over the post's machine (`Machine`), which the configurator binds to a face over the safe's reads (PST-1..PST-4, PST-10); the post's mailbox port (`MailboxPort`) and the operation of a deposit at the relay (PST-10, PST-11).
- [`requests/`](requests/), the business: `send_a_request`, the port of requests' call, which deposits the letter (`deposit_a_letter`, whose world is the post's mailbox port, GR-6, its delivery the dsl's `the_deposit_of_a_letter`, PST-10) and sends it again at each quarter of its time (`wait_to_send_again`, returned to `send_a_request_again`); `take_a_response`, from the kernel's entry; `close_a_request_with_no_response`, the return of `wait_for_the_response` at the end of its time; the read `the_answer_to_a_request`, the answer the port keeps (PST-5..PST-8); `call_off_a_request` and the read `the_requests_waiting`, the kernel's at a deactivation or a withdrawal (PST-14).
- No persona: the kernel's port of requests (step 12.4), the kernel's entry (step 13) and the effect executor reach the use cases, wired by the configurator (GR-6). The response on the server's side is no fact and no effect (F5, PST-9).

## Stories to prove

Each story is the test named after it (part 10.2), in `packages/memory/test/post/` (`stories_of_the_post`, PST-13), run over the safe's fake primitives port and over libsodium's. The stories of the protocol (1–6) run on the post's `dsl` through its generated `_dsl.ts` (MBX-5), with a face known by its private key (`a_face`): sign and open by the safe's dsl over the port, `sealed_for` and `the_signature_holds` of the safe's `dsl`, `hash` its BLAKE2b. The stories of the requests (7–17) run on q-'s oracle (MBX-7), its module `packages/memory/src/oracle/post.ts` on the model of `bonds.ts`, a phone's cell holding the requests with the safe; the deliveries of `deposit_a_letter` wired to a mailbox port over the relay's oracle (`packages/memory/test/relay/harness.ts`), with loss, duplication, abort and a relay's refusal injected on it; the port of requests and the kernel's entry played by the harness as steps 12.4 and 13 will wire them (`send_a_request` under the identity, `take_a_response` with an opened response). The names: Anna's phone and Bruno's, A and B their faces' keys, DA and DB the deposit addresses of their mailboxes of the bond, on relay R; Carla's face C, with no bond to Anna; P1 and P2 two requests of Anna's ping pong (`ask_for_a_pong`, entries P1 and P2), 60 seconds each.

1. **A letter goes in an envelope and comes out the same** (`a letter sealed for a face is opened by it with its sender as Caller`). Anna's request P1 to DB, put in an envelope for B by her face (`a_letter_in_an_envelope`): Bruno's face opens it at DB (`the_letter_in_an_envelope`) to the same letter, its `Caller` the face A (`a_face`). The signature is the fake's of A over `q-letter/` then the letter's canonical JSON; the envelope is format 1, its text base64url.
2. **A letter re-addressed fails** (`a letter re-addressed to another mailbox goes no further`). Bruno takes Anna's signed letter out of its envelope, seals it again for Carla and deposits it at Carla's D: Carla's face opens it, the signature holds, and the outcome is `re_addressed` (signed by A), never `opened`. The same letter at DB is `opened`.
3. **A signature removed or broken is `anonymous`** (`a letter with no signature or a broken one is anonymous`). The signature removed: `opened`, `anonymous`, `signature: 'none'`; a signature of another face, or the letter changed after signing (its input, its D): `anonymous`, `does_not_hold`, `claimed_face` A (PER-2). Never a `Caller` A. Its id is the hash of its envelope's text (PER-4).
4. **The envelope's hash is stable** (`the same envelope has the same id, another envelope another`). The id of an `anonymous` letter, for the same envelope text twice: the same; for the same letter in another envelope (sealed for another face): another id (PER-4: a different envelope, a new operation); sealed again for the same face, another id exactly when the sealed box is other bytes (libsodium's draws a new key; the fake's does not).
5. **Nothing comes out of what is no letter** (`what is no envelope, not sealed for the face or no letter is told why`). A text that is no base64url, base64url of no JSON, an envelope of format 2: `not_an_envelope`; an envelope sealed for A opened by Bruno's face, or its sealed box broken: `not_opened`; a sealed content that is no signed letter (a letter of schema 2, a field more, a request with no `invokes`): `not_a_letter`. A key that takes no seal (32 zero bytes, SEA-5): `a_letter_in_an_envelope` gives `not_sealed`.
6. **Two effects keyed by one value are two requests** (`the identity of a request is whose it is`). The identity of `{ of: 'an_extension', extension: 'ping_pong', effect: E1, id: 'x' }` and of the same with effect E2, or with another extension, or `{ of: 'the_kernel', effect: E1, id: 'x' }`: four identities, each 43 characters; the same twice, the same identity (F7). The identity of the response to P1 is neither P1's nor another request's.
7. **A request is sent** (`a request is sent and waits within its time`). The port sends P1 from A to B at DB, 60 seconds, at 10:00:00: `a_request_has_been_sent`, the letter's declared time 10:00:00, its time over at 10:01:00; the entries `deposit_a_letter` (P1/0), `wait_to_send_again` (P1/1, 10:00:15), `wait_for_the_response` (P1, 10:01:00). Delivered, the relay keeps one envelope at DB, and Bruno's face opens it to P1, `Caller` A. `the_answer_to_a_request` (P1): `waiting`. An input of more than 32 KiB of JSON: `invalid_request`, nothing sent; one of 32 KiB: deposited, its envelope within what the relay keeps (PST-12).
8. **A request is sent again while it waits** (`a request that waits is sent again at each quarter, the same letter`). No response: at 10:00:15, 10:00:30, 10:00:45 the same letter is deposited again (`a_request_has_been_sent_again`, attempts 1 to 3): opened, each is the same letter, under one identity. At 10:01:00 the request closes with no response; the wait of 10:01:00 for the fourth sending records `the_time_to_send_again_came_too_late`, nothing deposited. Answered at 10:00:20, the sending of 10:00:30 records `the_time_to_send_again_came_too_late` too.
9. **A response answers its request** (`a response from the face the request went to answers it once`). At 10:00:05 the entry gives `take_a_response` (P1, from B, `pong`) under the identity of the response to P1: `a_request_has_been_answered`, the answer `answered`, `pong`. The same response again (a copy, for a copy of the request): the recorded fact, no second fact, nothing refused. A response naming P1 with another event: `identity_already_used`. At 10:01:00, `wait_for_the_response` records `the_response_came_before_the_time`.
10. **A response from another face, or to no request, is refused** (`a response not awaited is refused`). From C naming P1: refused, `the_request_went_to_this_face`; naming a request Anna never sent: refused, `the_request_is_waiting`; after P1 had no response: refused, `the_request_is_waiting`. No fact; what the entry traces is step 13's.
11. **No response in its time** (`a request with no response in its time closes with no response`). Bruno's phone off: at 10:01:00 `a_request_had_no_response`, the answer `no_response`. His pong, coming at 10:01:30: refused (story 10).
12. **The answer is kept** (`a request called again reads the answer kept`). The port's call for P1 again under its identity, after a fall, while it waits, answered, or with no response: the recorded fact (DOM-11), its time the first call's (10:01:00), no second deposit; `the_answer_to_a_request` gives the answer as it stands. Two calls, P1 and P2, are two requests.
13. **A deposit lost, doubled or aborted** (`the deposits of a request survive loss, duplication and abort`). The transport loses P1/0 before the relay (`unreachable`, `transient`): the executor deposits it again; then the relay keeps it and its answer is lost: deposited again under the same operation, `kept_already` (a new sealed box) or the relay's recorded fact (the same bytes), one envelope (PST-11); the transport sends P1/1 twice: one envelope at the relay (MBX-1); an abort while depositing P1/2 stops the effect, unadvanced, and started again it deposits it (DOM-14). Bruno's phone collects three copies of one letter, and his duplicate branch meets one operation (DOM-11). Anna's record holds one request, answered once. With every fact and entry given twice (TST-4), each sending is one envelope at the relay, and the post holds no dead letter.
14. **A response lost is asked again** (`a response lost comes back with a sending again`). Bruno's phone answers P1 at 10:00:01, the deposit of its response lost (no effect, F5); at 10:00:15 Anna sends P1 again, Bruno answers from his recorded fact (the harness plays his duplicate branch), the same response, which answers P1. One pong.
15. **A deposit that cannot be sealed is a dead letter, shown** (`a letter for a key that takes no seal is a dead letter`). The bond's key takes no seal (SEA-5): `deposit_a_letter` P1/0 is `exhausted`, `their_key_takes_no_seal`, a dead letter of the post's executor, shown (F6); its sendings again too; at 10:01:00 the request closes with no response. No fact of the dead letter (DOM-15). A relay that refuses the envelope: `exhausted`, `the_relay_refused_the_envelope` with what it said (PST-10).
16. **A phone closed while a request waits** (`a phone started again closes its requests from the record`; the post's part of the gate's check of DOM-14). Anna asks Bruno (P1, his phone off) and Carla (P2); Carla's pong is taken; Anna's phone is closed before P1's time and started again at 10:02:00: P1 closes with no response, P2's answer is read from the record (`answered`), and the port's call for P2 met again is the recorded fact, nothing deposited; one pong, no dead letter.
17. **A request called off is sent again no more** (`a request called off keeps its refusal and is sent again no more`; PST-14, A's decision of 21 Nov 2026). Anna asks Bruno (P1) and Carla (P2); `the_requests_waiting` gives both, with what they invoke, her face and the key each went to. At 10:00:10 she withdraws her ping pong from her bond with Carla: the kernel calls P2 off, `not_offered_to_the_bond`, `a_request_has_been_called_off`, its answer `called_off`; called off again under its operation, the recorded fact; under another, refused, `the_request_is_waiting`. P2 is deposited no more (one copy at Carla's mailbox, four at Bruno's), its time records `the_call_off_came_before_the_time`, P1 closes with no response, and Carla's pong after is refused.
18. **A request called off is sent again no more** (`a request called off keeps its refusal and is sent again no more`; PST-14, A's decision of 21 Nov 2026). Anna asks Bruno (P1) and Carla (P2); `the_requests_waiting` gives both, with what they invoke, her face and the key each went to. At 10:00:10 she withdraws her ping pong from her bond with Carla: the kernel calls P2 off, `not_offered_to_the_bond`, `a_request_has_been_called_off`, its answer `called_off`; called off again under its operation, the recorded fact; under another, refused, `the_request_is_waiting`. P2 is deposited no more (one copy at Carla's mailbox, four at Bruno's), its time records `the_call_off_came_before_the_time`, P1 closes with no response, and Carla's pong after is refused.

The checks of step 10 not here are of the steps that wire the rest: "a request redelivered gives the recorded response, no second fact" on the server is the leaf's duplicate branch through the kernel's entry (step 13, PER-5; stories 13 and 14 play it); "an exhausted delivery becomes a dead letter and is shown" for an extension's request is EXT-12's, its delivery mapping the port's answer (step 12.4, 14). Of the gate's checks on the request and the response: the deliveries of an effect `order: 'any'` in flight together and the executor's position behind the oldest (DOM-14, F7) are the library's (12.3), the port's answer kept and read by a delivery met again the port of requests' (12.4: story 16 proves the record it reads); a response that does not parse into the return's closed set is the extension's delivery (EXT-12, 12.4, 14).
