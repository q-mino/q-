# The bonds: `bonds`

The address book of the node's user: her faces, each with its name, its place in her tree of faces and its public key, and her bonds, each one of her faces with their face, their mailbox and hers, the name she gives them and what her node noted of the handshake. It holds no secret: the keys are the safe's, which it names by their opaque references. Its rules are BND-1..BND-12 of [`architecture.md`](../../../../docs/architecture.md), with DOM-23, GR-9, PER-11, PER-21, DOM-12 and the invariants of § 4; its words in the [glossary](../../../../docs/glossary.md).

- [`address_book/`](address_book/), the domain (BND-1): the faces (being made, then made, BND-3) and the bonds (kept, then removed, BND-4..BND-6); `rename_a_bond` and `remove_a_bond`, what the user keeps of them; the reads `my_faces`, `my_bonds`, `a_bond` and `the_bond_at_my_mailbox`. Since part 11.2 the processes that make them (BND-7..BND-11): the first face (`make_my_first_face`, `keep_the_key_of_a_face`), the ticket, a bond's first stage (`prepare_a_ticket`, `keep_the_mailbox_of_a_ticket`, `let_a_ticket_expire` after the wait of 48 hours), the introduction from both sides (`open_their_ticket` with the effect `send_my_introduction`, `accept_their_introduction`); the reads `my_ticket`, `the_ticket_at_my_mailbox`, `the_mailboxes_i_collect`.
- [`../reaction/`](../reaction/), the procedures toward the safe (GR-9, BND-12): `first_face/` (the safe makes the face, the address book keeps its key) and `ticket/` (the safe draws the key of the ticket's mailbox, the address book keeps its D).

## Stories to prove

Each story is the test named after it (part 11.3), in `packages/memory/test/bonds/stories.ts`, on q-'s oracle (MBX-7: a module `oracle/bonds.ts` on the model of `seals.ts`, a cell of the phone's with the state of `bonds` and `seals`), entering the address book's use cases as the user's entry would (`self`, step 13), the node's clock and the operations injected (TST-2, TST-3). The faces and bonds the stories start from are made as part 11.2's stories make them, through the first start and the introduction.

The names: Anna's phone has her face F_a, "Anna", at the top, its public key K_a. Monday 10:00, by her clock, she and Bruno crossed their QR codes: bond L_b, Bruno's face K_b introduced as "Bruno", his mailbox D_b on the relay `https://relay.q-mino.test`, hers for him at D_mb (its key in the safe under L_b, BND-7) on the same relay, in person, the confirmation received at 10:00. Tuesday 18:30 she opened Carla's link: bond L_c, K_c "Carla", her mailbox D_c, Anna's for her at D_mc, at a distance, the confirmation awaited.

1. **My faces** (`my faces are the made ones, with their place and key`). While the safe makes F_a, `my_faces` holds nothing; once it is made, one entry: F_a, "Anna", at the top, K_a.
2. **My list** (`my bonds are listed by the name I give, each with one face of mine`). `my_bonds` holds L_b "Bruno" and L_c "Carla", in that order, each with F_a, their face (K_b introduced as "Bruno"; K_c as "Carla") and the confirmation: received at 10:00 for Bruno, awaited for Carla.
3. **A bond, whole** (`a bond reads whole, their mailbox and mine`). `a_bond` for L_b: "Bruno", F_a, K_b introduced as "Bruno", his mailbox D_b on its one relay, hers at D_mb on its one relay, the handshake in person at Monday 10:00, the confirmation received at 10:00.
4. **A name of mine** (`a bond renamed keeps the name they are introduced as`). Anna renames L_b "Bru": `a_bond_has_been_renamed`; `my_bonds` lists "Bru" then "Carla", and L_b's face is still introduced as "Bruno". Nothing is deposited for Bruno: the name is hers. Sent again under the same operation, the rename is the recorded fact (`a rename sent again is the same rename`, DOM-11).
5. **The bond of a letter** (`a letter at my mailbox finds its bond, the face that opens it and the key that signs it`). `the_bond_at_my_mailbox` at D_mb gives L_b, F_a and K_b; at D_mc, L_c, F_a and K_c; at D_b, Bruno's mailbox and not hers, nothing (`an address of no mailbox of mine finds no bond`).
6. **Removed** (`a bond removed leaves the list, and its mailbox finds it no more`). Wednesday 9:00 Anna removes L_c: `a_bond_has_been_removed`, removed at 9:00 by her clock; `my_bonds` holds L_b alone, `a_bond` for L_c nothing, `the_bond_at_my_mailbox` at D_mc nothing. Nothing is deposited for Carla. Removed again: `a_bond_has_been_removed_again`, and nothing changes (`a bond removed again changes nothing`). Renamed after: refused, `the_bond_is_kept` failed, no fact (`a bond removed cannot be renamed`).
7. **Exactly one face** (`every bond belongs to exactly one of my faces`). Every entry of `my_bonds` and `the_bond_at_my_mailbox` names one face, which is in `my_faces`; a bond with no face, or with a list of faces, is not a bond of the address book: its schema refuses it. That a bond is made only on a made face of mine is part 11.2's story.
8. **What the address book does not take** (`a name empty or too long, or an address out of its form, is not taken`). A rename to "" or to 81 characters, and `the_bond_at_my_mailbox` at a D with a character out of base64url or over 128 characters, are `invalid_request` before any node; nothing is recorded. A relay that is not an `https` URL, or two relays on a mailbox, are not a bond's (the schemas of their mailbox and mine): part 11.2's introduction meets them.
9. **No secret** (`no read of the address book gives a secret`). The fields the four queries declare hold ids, names, positions, public keys, deposit addresses, relay addresses and annotations: no collect secret, no private key, no chain code, no seed, no index (BND-2, SEA-6).

### The first face, the ticket and the introduction (part 11.2)

On the oracle a phone runs `bonds`, `seals` and the reactions of `reaction/`, with the safe's executor and the address book's (its waits), over the safe's fake primitives (SEA-8); the phone's clock and the operations injected. Anna's phone and Bruno's, each its own cell; a letter of introduction goes from one to the other by hand (the stories of `post`, step 10, carry it on the relay): the test takes the payload of `send_my_introduction` and gives the other phone's `accept_their_introduction` what its entry would (step 13): the bond of the ticket at whose mailbox it came, the secret, the signer's key, the mailbox its ticket declares. The relay is `https://relay.q-mino.test`.

10. **The first face** (`the first start makes my face, its key from the safe`). Anna's phone, at its first start, makes F_a "Anna": `a_first_face_has_been_asked_for`; `reaction/first_face` asks the safe for the face under F_a, the safe's executor draws its seed, and the reaction back keeps its public key: `a_face_has_been_made`, and `my_faces` holds F_a, "Anna", at the top, K_a, the safe's key of F_a. A second first face is refused, `the_user_has_a_face` (`a second first face is refused`).
11. **A ticket ready** (`a ticket is ready once the safe drew its mailbox, for 48 hours`). Monday 10:00 Anna asks for the ticket of L_b on F_a, on the relay: `a_ticket_has_been_asked_for`, expiring Wednesday 10:00, and its wait queued for then; while the safe draws, `my_ticket` for L_b holds nothing. `reaction/ticket` asks the safe for the key of the mailbox under L_b, and the reaction back keeps its D, D_mb: `a_ticket_has_been_prepared`; `my_ticket` gives K_a, "Anna", D_mb on the relay, until Wednesday 10:00, and `the_mailboxes_i_collect` holds L_b at D_mb. A ticket on a face not made, or under the id of a bond there already, is refused (`a ticket on a face not made is refused`).
12. **The ticket's secret** (`a ticket's secret is my face's signature over its mailbox`). The secret of L_b's ticket is the safe's signature by F_a of `dsl.the_content_a_ticket_s_secret_signs(D_mb)`; `the_secret_is_the_ticket_s` holds for it, and not for a signature of the same content by another face, nor for the secret of another ticket of hers.
13. **The crossed QR, Anna's phone** (`crossed QR: I open theirs, their introduction comes within two minutes, in person`). Monday 10:00 Bruno's ticket T_b (K_b, "Bruno", D_b on the relay) and Anna's of L_b are on the two screens. 10:00:05 Anna's phone opens T_b, presenting L_b: `a_bond_has_been_made_by_opening_their_ticket`, L_b with K_b introduced as "Bruno", his mailbox D_b on the relay, named "Bruno", at a distance at 10:00:05, the confirmation awaited, her introduction sent, and `send_my_introduction` queued for L_b with T_b. 10:00:20 Bruno's introduction to L_b's ticket comes, with its secret: `their_introduction_has_come_in_person`, in person at 10:00:20, the confirmation received at 10:00:20.
14. **The crossed QR, Bruno's phone** (`crossed QR: their introduction comes first, I open theirs within two minutes, in person`). 10:00:10 Anna's introduction to his ticket comes first: `a_bond_has_been_made_by_their_introduction`, named "Anna", at a distance, received, his introduction not sent. 10:00:30 his phone opens Anna's ticket, presenting his: `their_ticket_has_been_opened_in_person`, in person at 10:00:30, his introduction sent, `send_my_introduction` queued. Then `the crossed QR is one bond, the same on both phones`: on each, one bond with the other, their face and mailbox the other's ticket's, crosswise (Anna deposits on the D Bruno collects, and the reverse), in person, received, sent; and `the_mailboxes_i_collect` on each holds its ticket's mailbox, now the bond's.
15. **A link** (`a link opened makes a bond at a distance, awaiting their confirmation`). Tuesday 18:30 Anna opens Carla's link T_c (K_c, "Carla", D_c), presenting L_c's ticket, prepared for it: L_c at a distance, awaited, her introduction sent. On Carla's phone her introduction to Carla's ticket makes Carla's bond, "Anna", at a distance, received; Carla's introduction not sent, and Anna's confirmation stays awaited (its letter: step 13).
16. **Crossed later** (`tickets crossed later than two minutes stay at a distance`). The second event at 10:03: `their_introduction_has_come` (the confirmation received) or `their_ticket_has_been_opened_after_their_introduction` (her introduction sent), and the handshake stays at a distance.
17. **Again** (`an opening or an introduction again changes nothing`). Bruno's ticket opened again: `their_ticket_has_been_opened_again`, no introduction queued; his introduction again: `their_introduction_has_come_again`. Sent again under the same operation, each is its recorded fact (DOM-11).
18. **A ticket used twice** (`a ticket used by another face is refused`). Carla's introduction to L_b's ticket, used by Bruno: refused, no fact, `the_ticket_is_ready` and `the_bond_is_with_this_face` failed; on a bond removed, refused the same (BND-6).
19. **A secret not its** (`an introduction with a secret not the ticket's is refused`). An introduction to L_c's ready ticket with a secret of another ticket, or of another face: refused, `the_secret_is_the_ticket_s` failed, no bond.
20. **Expired** (`a ticket unused for 48 hours expires, and is refused after`). Wednesday 10:00 the wait of an unused ticket comes back: `a_ticket_has_expired`; `my_ticket` and `the_mailboxes_i_collect` hold it no more, and an introduction to it is refused. The wait of L_b's ticket, used Monday: `the_ticket_was_used_before_it_expired`, L_b kept. A mailbox key coming for an expired ticket: `a_mailbox_has_come_for_an_expired_ticket`, still expired.
21. **The relays declared** (`a bond's relays are those its peer declared`). On Anna's phone L_b's mailbox of Bruno's is D_b on the relay his ticket declared; on Bruno's, Anna's is the one her introduction declared; a later introduction declaring another changes nothing. A ticket whose relay is not an `https` URL, or with two relays, is `invalid_request` before any node (`a ticket with a relay out of its form is not taken`).
