# The secrets: `seals`

The safe of the node: it keeps the seeds, the faces' keys and the mailboxes' collect secrets, and lets out only public keys, ids, and what a face computes with its private key (a signature, a content opened). Its machine is the primitives port, libsodium (step 18). Its rules are SEA-1..SEA-7 of [`architecture.md`](../../../../docs/architecture.md), with GR-5, GR-8, DOM-8, DOM-21, PER-21 and the invariants of § 4; its words in the [glossary](../../../../docs/glossary.md).

- [`safe/`](safe/), the domain: `make_a_face_from_a_new_seed`, whose seed and face's keys the machine draws and derives (`draw_a_seed_for_the_face`), kept by its return `keep_a_new_seed_and_its_face` (SEA-2, SEA-3); `draw_a_mailbox_key`, R drawn and D its BLAKE2b, kept by `keep_a_mailbox_key` (SEA-4); the reads `a_content_signed_by_a_face`, `a_sealed_content_opened_by_a_face` (calculations over the face, SEA-6) and `the_key_of_a_mailbox` (R and D, toward the relay, V5). In `dsl.ts`: the primitives port `Primitives`, SLIP-0010's `the_face_at`, the deliveries of the effects, `sealed_for` and `the_signature_holds` (SEA-5, SEA-6).
- No persona: the commands are reached by reactions and by the effect executor only, wired by the configurator (PER-21).

## Stories to prove

Each story is the test named after it (part 9.2). The stories of the core (1–4) run on the safe's `dsl.ts` with a **fake primitives port** that records its calls and answers by a rule the test knows (an HMAC that is a function of its key and data, a public key that is a function of its private key, a signature that is a function of both, a sealed box that names its public key, BLAKE2b a function of its data, random bytes from a list): proposed in `packages/domains/test/seals/core.test.ts`, through the generated `_dsl.ts` (MBX-5). The stories of the safe (5–12) run on q-'s oracle (MBX-7), with a module `packages/memory/src/oracle/seals.ts` on the model of `mailbox.ts`, its fragments of the calculations computed with the dsl over the fake port, the effect executor delivering the draws with `dsl.a_new_seed_and_its_first_face` and `dsl.a_new_mailbox_key`, and the use cases invoked as a reaction and the executor do (PER-21): proposed in `packages/memory/test/seals/stories.ts`. The SLIP-0010 vectors run in step 18, with the real HMAC. The names: Anna's phone; F1 and F2 her faces, as `bonds` names them; M1 a mailbox key; C1 a content.

1. **SLIP-0010, one level** (`the face at an index is the hardened child of the seed`). The fake port's HMAC is called first under the key "ed25519 seed" over the seed's bytes, then under the master's chain code (its right half) over 0x00, the master's key (its left half) and the four bytes of 2³¹ + i, the most significant first; the face's private key is the left half of the second, its chain code the right half, its public key the port's of the private key.
2. **The same face again** (`the same seed and index give the same face, another index another`). `the_face_at` of one seed at index 3, twice: the same keys; at index 4: other keys. Of another seed at 3: other keys.
3. **Bytes in base64url** (`the values go round base64url`). Bytes of 0 to 64, and 32 and 64 in particular, told by `base64url_of` and read back by `bytes_of`, are the same bytes; a 32-byte value is 43 characters and passes `value.public_key`, a 64-byte one 86 and passes `value.signature`.
4. **Sealing and verifying need no secret** (`a content is sealed for a public key and a signature checked by it`). `sealed_for` calls the port's sealed box with the public key's bytes and the content's; `the_signature_holds` the port's check, and is false for another content.
5. **Anna's first face** (`the first face is made from a new seed`). A reaction asks `make_a_face_from_a_new_seed` for F1: `a_face_has_been_asked_for` (F1 being made, no seed, no keys) and the entry `draw_a_seed_for_the_face` (F1). The executor delivers it, the fake port drawing the seed S: the return records `a_face_has_been_made_from_a_new_seed`, the seed (S's bytes, `slip10-flat`, next index 1, its id the return's operation's) and F1 made at index 0, its keys `the_face_at(S, 0)`.
6. **Asked twice, one face** (`a face asked for again is one face`). The reaction's request for F1 again, under its operation: the recorded fact (DOM-11), one entry. A request under another operation naming F1: rejected, `a_face_has_this_id`.
7. **A draw delivered twice** (`a draw delivered again keeps one seed`). The executor delivers `draw_a_seed_for_the_face` twice (TST-4): the return's second delivery is the duplicate branch, one seed, one face. A return for a face not being made (F2 never asked for): rejected, `the_face_is_being_made`, a dead letter.
8. **A mailbox key** (`a mailbox key is drawn with its deposit address`). A reaction asks `draw_a_mailbox_key` for M1: asked for, then, the executor delivering, `a_mailbox_key_has_been_drawn` with R drawn and D the fake BLAKE2b of R. `the_key_of_a_mailbox` (M1) gives M1, R and D; D fits the form of the mailbox's deposit address (MBX-1).
9. **A signature that holds** (`a content signed by a face holds for its public key`). `a_content_signed_by_a_face` (F1, C1) gives F1, its public key and its signature over C1, the fake port's with F1's private key; `the_signature_holds` with that public key over C1 is true, over another content false. Nothing is recorded by the read.
10. **Sealed for one face** (`a content sealed for a face is opened by it alone`). C1 sealed for F1's public key: `a_sealed_content_opened_by_a_face` (F1) gives it opened, C1; by F2, made from another seed, `not_opened`; a sealed content broken, `not_opened`.
11. **A face being made reads nothing** (`a face not made signs and opens nothing`). Before its draw is delivered, F1 signs nothing and opens nothing: the reads give no entry; nor does a face never asked for.
12. **No secret leaves** (`no read of the safe gives a secret`). The entries of `a_content_signed_by_a_face` and `a_sealed_content_opened_by_a_face` hold only the face's id, its public key and the calculation; no read gives a private key, a chain code, a seed or an index, by the fields their queries declare; `the_key_of_a_mailbox` gives R and D only (V5). What is invalid is not taken (`a value out of its form is not taken`): a content not base64url, a public key of 31 bytes, are `invalid_request`.
