# The user: `user`

The user domain of the phone (D76, ADP-7, GR-7): what the user asks her node, in her words, and what she is told of what it did. It sees no other domain (`sight.ts`, `user: {}`): it names faces, bonds and extensions by the identities `self`'s reads give, and its port toward `self`, the configurator's, is typed on `self`'s contract. Its rules are USR-1..USR-9 of [`architecture.md`](../../../../docs/architecture.md), with GR-2, GR-7, ADP-2, ADP-7, DOM-12, DOM-15, LIB-12, F6, EXT-7, EXT-15, EXT-16; its words in the [glossary](../../../../docs/glossary.md).

- [`value.ts`](value.ts), [`dsl.ts`](dsl.ts): her words shared by the offices: a face, a bond, an extension; what a leaf said, as data; her view of a manifest and **what an extension asks** (USR-7); a **held letter** and **what happened without a fact** (USR-8).
- [`activities/`](activities/), **the activities** (_le attività_, USR-2..USR-4): `ask_for_an_activity` (its id drawn before the first attempt, and what is asked, an ADT: a gesture of the kernel's screens, an extension's action as data, a gesture on a held letter), carried out by the effect `carry_out_an_activity` (`order: 'any'`, the library's queue, LIB-12) through the port toward `self`, and ended by its return `end_an_activity`, with how it ended in her words; the reads `an_activity` and `the_activities_in_progress`.
- [`drafts/`](drafts/), **the drafts** (_le bozze_, USR-5): `keep_a_draft`, `drop_a_draft`, `a_draft`, by what it is for.
- [`choices/`](choices/), **the choices** (_le scelte_, USR-6): `change_face`, `the_active_face`.
- Its adapters (USR-9, part 15.2), in [`adapters-common/src/user/`](../../../adapters-common/src/user/): **the port toward `self`**, the delivery of `carry_out_an_activity` (`the_delivery_of_an_activity`, its link to `self`'s events a type), and the reads of `self` passing through; **the news**, held letters and what happened without a fact in her words. On the oracle: [`memory/src/oracle/user.ts`](../../../memory/src/oracle/user.ts), its three offices in the phone's cell.

## Stories to prove

Each story is the test named after it, in `packages/memory/test/user/user.test.ts` (part 15.2, proven: 20 tests), on q-'s oracle ([`harness.ts`](../../../memory/test/user/harness.ts)): Anna's phone of the node's harness (`packages/memory/test/node/harness.ts`, its cast in `annas_phone.ts`) with a cell for `user`'s state beside the kernel's, `user`'s executor delivering `carry_out_an_activity` through the port toward `self` (the tree by path as the user, `self/the_user`, and an extension's `self` through its hosting), the node's clock and the activities' ids injected (TST-2, TST-3); stories 15 and 16 on the dsl alone. Each executor with a real timer, each run inside an outer `timeout`.

The names: Anna's phone, her face F_a, her bonds L_b with Bruno and L_c with Carla; Bruno's phone; ping pong 0.1.0 preinstalled (its action `ping` on a bond, its leaf `ping` on known faces), activated on both phones unless a story says otherwise. Friday 20 November 2026, 10:00 in Rome. A1, A2… are activities, their ids drawn by the story.

1. **Asked** (`an activity asked is in progress, its carrying out queued`). Anna asks A1, to rename L_b "Bruno B.": `an_activity_has_been_asked`, A1 among `the_activities_in_progress`, its stage `in_progress`, one entry of `carry_out_an_activity` keyed by A1. Asked again with A1 (a tap repeated): `an_activity_has_been_asked_again`, one entry still.
2. **Done** (`a kernel's activity is carried out on self under its id, and ends done with what the leaf said`). A1 is carried out: `self`'s `rename_a_bond` invoked under the operation A1 (`self/the_user`) says `the_bond_is_renamed`; A1 ends `done`, with that event as data; no longer in progress; `my_bonds` shows L_b "Bruno B.".
3. **Each event in her words** (`each event of a kernel's leaf ends as the port's map says`). One case per row of USR-3's map that the cast can reach: L_b removed twice (`done`, then `done_already`); L_x renamed (`cannot_be_done`, `there_is_no_such_bond`); ping pong activated twice (`done`, `done_already`); deactivated while off (`done_already`); the offer withdrawn from L_c twice (`done`, `done_already`).
4. **The manifest shown** (`activating with the manifest shown ends done; one shown before an update cannot be done`). Anna activates ping pong with the manifest she was shown: `done`, ping pong active. On a phone whose ping pong is 0.2.0 (it also sees the bond's name), the manifest of 0.1.0: `cannot_be_done`, `the_manifest_is_not_the_installed_one`; ping pong stays as it was.
5. **An extension's action** (`an action of ping pong on a bond is invoked on self with the bond, and ends with the extension's answer`). Anna asks A2, `use_an_action` `ping` of ping pong on L_b: the port finds the action's leaf in the composed manifest (`usecase/send_a_ping`), invokes it on ping pong's `self` with `{ bond: L_b }`, follows `the_ping_is_sent` to `pong`; A2 ends `the_extension_answered`, `pong`. Bruno's phone off: after the ping's 60 seconds A3 ends `the_extension_answered`, `no_response`.
6. **No longer followed** (`a leaf's wait over before its end ends no longer followed`). A ping whose closing does not come within the screen's 70 seconds (the story holds Bruno's pong and ends the time): the leaf says `the_wait_is_over`, A4 ends `no_longer_followed`; the ping's own closing comes later in ping pong's partition, never in A4.
7. **Not reachable** (`an action of an extension not active, or stopped, ends the extension cannot be reached`). Ping pong deactivated, then A5 `ping` on L_b: `the_extension_cannot_be_reached`, `it_is_not_active`, nothing invoked. Ping pong fallen twice: A6 ends `it_has_stopped`.
8. **A fall in between** (`a fall while an activity is carried out is tried again under the same id, and meets the recorded fact`). Ping pong falls once while A7 (`ping` on L_b) is in flight (its partition refuses the ping's commit): the delivery is transient, A7 still in progress; tried again after the executor's pause under A7: one ping, named by A7, one pong, A7 ends once. Her app closed while A8 is in flight, its ping recorded: opened again, the executor meets A8 again, and ping pong's duplicate branch gives the ping it recorded: one ping more, one pong more.
9. **In flight together** (`an activity that waits holds no other behind it`). Bruno's phone off, Anna asks A8 (`ping` on L_b) then A9 (rename L_c): A9 ends `done` while A8 is in progress; A8 ends at the ping's time.
10. **The app's fault** (`a base event of self ends the app cannot do it`). An activity whose input `self`'s schema refuses (their ticket with a key `self` does not take): `invalid_request`, A10 ends `the_app_cannot_do_it` with it; the port speaking a version of `self` the node does not (9.0.0): `version_not_supported`, the same.
11. **An end refused** (`an end for an activity not in progress is refused, a held letter of user`). `end_an_activity` for A1, ended already, with another how: refused, `the_activity_is_in_progress` failed, a dead letter of `user`'s executor, told as a held letter (story 17); A1 keeps its first end.
12. **A draft kept** (`a draft is kept by what it is for, and read back`). Anna types "Bruno" for the bond L_b she is opening: `a_draft_has_been_kept`; `a_draft` for `the_name_i_give_a_bond` L_b gives "Bruno"; the same text again, `a_draft_has_been_kept_again`; "Bruno B.", kept, the new text read.
13. **A draft dropped** (`a draft dropped is no longer read; none kept, nothing changes`). The bond opened, the draft dropped: `a_draft_has_been_dropped`, `a_draft` gives nothing; dropped again, `a_draft_has_been_dropped_again`. The draft of her first face's name is another.
14. **The active face** (`no active face before the first, then the one she changed to`). Before her first face, `the_active_face` gives nothing; F_a made, `change_face` F_a: `the_active_face_has_been_changed`, F_a since 10:00; F_a again, `the_active_face_has_been_changed_again`.
15. **What ping pong asks** (`what an extension asks, in the order the notice tells it`). Ping pong's manifest: `to_send_requests_on_your_bonds`, `a_storage_of_its_own`, `to_answer_your_bonds` (`usecase/ping`), `a_place_on_your_screens` (the action `ping` on `a_bond`), `the_network_as_far_as_the_browser_leaves_it`; no flaw of frames. A manifest that sees the bond's name and has a slot: `to_see_the_name_of_the_bond` first, and `its_frames_act_with_no_tap` last.
16. **Every base event named** (`how a closing ends: the wait over, the base events and the node's closings`). `the_wait_is_over` → `no_longer_followed`; `invalid_request`, `version_not_supported`, `identity_already_used`, `not_found`, `refused` → `the_app_cannot_do_it`; a leaf's own event → none (the port's map decides).
17. **A held letter** (`a held letter of ping pong is told in her words, and sent again`). Anna removes L_b, then pings it (the ping's delivery goes at once on the oracle): ping pong's `dead_letter` (the bond gone) is told as a held letter of `ping_pong`, `it_could_not_be_delivered`; A11 `send_a_held_letter_again` hands it to ping pong (`redeliver`) and ends `done`; held again, it is told again.
18. **Put aside** (`a held letter put aside is no longer held`). A12 `put_a_held_letter_aside` with her reason: ping pong `discard`s it, A12 ends `done`; asked again for it, `done_already`.
19. **Silence, told her** (`a silence of her node is told her with its case`). Anna withdraws ping pong's offer from L_c; Carla's ping meets silence (F8) and Anna is told `a_request_met_silence`, ping pong, `the_extension_is_not_offered_to_the_bond`; the same with ping pong deactivated, `the_extension_is_not_active`.
20. **The kernel's trace in her words** (`each reason of the kernel's trace is told in her words`). One case per reason of the kernel's trace (EXT-6) and a tree's trace entry (PER-14), each told as USR-8 links it.
