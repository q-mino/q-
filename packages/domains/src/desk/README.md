# `desk`: the relay's personas

The counter of the relay's post office (PER-19): `depositor`, anyone, deposits; `holder`, who shows the collect secret R of a mailbox, collects it; since part B4 of the assistant, `inviter`, who signs with a stand's key, leaves invitations on that stand, and `passer_by`, anyone, reads a stand and takes an invitation (INV-5; the stand's rules in [`../stand/README.md`](../stand/README.md)). Its business, its rules and the stories to prove are in [`../mailbox/README.md`](../mailbox/README.md).
