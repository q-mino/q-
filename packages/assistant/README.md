# The assistant: `assistant`

**The assistant** (_l'assistente_, Alessandro's word) is a contact inside Cumino who answers questions about Cumino: a real node, with a face, bonds and messaging active and offered to every bond, running in Node on Alessandro's laptop, whose replies are written by Claude (Claude Code, `claude -p`, on Alessandro's subscription, no API key) inside a cage that sees only a read-only copy of the project's notes and of this repository's documents. It answers only about Cumino: how it works, questions and answers, feature requests, advice. It saves an **anonymous, rephrased note** of a request in the project's notes only when the writer asks it to. It answers while the laptop is on; while it is off, the messages wait on the relay and the reply comes later. A page on the relay's site, `https://relay.cumino.aleclofabbro.it/assistente`, gives each visitor a handshake link, taken from **the invitation stand** (_il banco degli inviti_), where the assistant leaves fresh tickets.

Its rules are AST-1..AST-16 and, for the stand on the relay, INV-1..INV-10 of [`architecture.md`](../../docs/architecture.md) (§ 7 "The assistant: `assistant`", § 4 "The stand: `stand`"); its words in the [glossary](../../docs/glossary.md) ("The words of the assistant and of the stand"). The UX it follows, as background and not as constraint: the project's notes, `scenari` § 6, `flussi` § 6, `schermate` § 8 (Alessandro's, in Italian).

**Its origin** [Q, C, 27 Nov 2026]: designed in part B1 of the night of 27–28 November 2026 (Alessandro, 27 Nov 2026: «un'altra sessione lunga, autonoma, per sviluppare e pubblicare nell'MVP il nodo con messaggistica bot. Ci sarai tu, Claude, a rispondere»; «Se invece delle API potessimo ridirezionare qui su Ubuntu, quando è acceso risponde, quando è spento no, risponderà più tardi»), built in parts B2–B6 (§ Parts below). The Italian words are provisional (`[Q, C, provisional: A to confirm]`, § Its words).

**Not in it**: the assistant as a special case of the kernel (it is a user of an ordinary node, AST-1); a screen of its own in the PWA (`flussi` § 6 has a screen before the bond, «Collega o No»: here the page says it, and the welcome repeats it, AST-5); a permanent link for many (`schermate` § 8: a ticket is one-time and expires in 48 hours, BND-8..BND-10, so the stand gives one per visitor, INV-1); notes taken without being asked (`scenari` § 6 had «L'ho annotata come idea»: Alessandro, 27 Nov 2026, «solo se chi scrive chiede di salvarle»); the news channel; groups.

## How it is made

- **The node** (AST-2): the kernel's phone in Node, STK-1's `phone(storage, release)(world)`, over a **persistent storage** on SQLite (`node:sqlite`, part B2), the relay's HTTP client (WEB-6), libsodium's primitives (SEA-9), the system clock in Europe/Rome; it carries **messaging** and **ping pong**, both activated at the first start and so offered to every bond, present and to come (EXT-7). Its face is «Assistente di Cumino».
- **The register** (_il registro_, AST-3): the assistant's own notebook, a SQLite file beside the node's, outside the kernel: the turns it is working on, the turns closed by day (for the caps), the tickets left by day. It is no tree of the generator: it keeps no business of a node, only a user's bookkeeping.
- **A turn** (_un turno_, AST-4): the messages of a bond not read yet (the chat's own `to_read`, MSG-7) are answered by **one reply** (_una risposta_), written in the chat; then the chat is marked read up to the last message the reply covers. What is unread is what is unanswered: the chat in the node's storage says it, so a restart and the replay of every start (K-6) answer nothing twice.
- **The answerer** (_chi risponde_, AST-7): a port, `answers(a turn) → an answer`, with two adapters: **`claude`**, Claude Code in **the cage** (AST-8, AST-9), and **`scripted`**, for the tests and for a run before Alessandro has logged in. Tomorrow an API key is a third adapter, chosen by a setting.
- **The cage** (_la gabbia_, AST-8): `bwrap`, with only the system's `/usr` read-only, the copy read-only, the bot's own Claude configuration (`~/.config/cumino-assistente/claude`) and a `/tmp` of its own; no home, no `~/.ssh`, no `~/.aws`, no vault. Inside it Claude Code has the tools `Read`, `Grep` and `Glob` and nothing else, and answers in a JSON of its own form, `{ reply, note? }`. **In the container** (28 Nov 2026, Alessandro's choice: the node in Docker), the container is the cage: its root read-only, no capability, Alessandro's uid, and only the folders the assistant needs mounted (`deploy/assistant/`); `bwrap` stays for a run outside it.
- **The copy** (_la copia_, AST-11): the files of the project's notes named by an **allow-list** (since part B3 the project's worktree `~/my-brain/wt/q/projects/q/`, where the notes are current; B1 had the master checkout), each checked against a list of words never copied, and the documents of this repository at the commit the assistant runs; refreshed at the start and once a day, swapped whole.
- **The notes** (_le note delle richieste_, AST-12): one Markdown file per request saved, in `projects/q/richieste/` of the project's worktree, with the vault's frontmatter, anonymous: no name, no bond, no verbatim quote, the day only.
- **The stand** (_il banco_, INV-1..INV-10, AST-13): on the relay, a stand named `assistente` keeps at most five invitations (a ticket's text and until when it can be taken); the assistant, signing with a key of its own whose public half is in the relay's configuration, tops it up at most twenty tickets a day; each visit of the page that asks for a link takes one away.

## Stories

The names: the assistant's node, «Assistente di Cumino», on Alessandro's laptop; Bea, a visitor, with Cumino on her Android phone, her bond with the assistant `B-ASSISTENTE`, the assistant's with her `B-BEA`; Thursday 28 November 2026 in Rome. Each story is the test named after it, in the part that proves it (§ Parts); the stand's own stories are INV-10's, on the relay (B4).

### The node (B3)

1. **The first start** (`the first start: the face made, messaging and ping pong active, the stand's key drawn, the copy made`). No storage in `~/.local/share/cumino-assistente/`: the node makes its first face «Assistente di Cumino» (BND-8), activates messaging and ping pong accepting their manifests as shown (EXT-7, USR-7), draws the stand's key pair (AST-13), and makes the copy (AST-11). One line in the journal: started, its version and commit, the answerer it uses, how many files the copy holds.
2. **The start again** (`started again over its storage: the same face, its bonds and chats, nothing made twice`). Stopped and started: the same face, the same bonds and chats, the same stand key; no face, no activation, no key made again.
3. **The stand topped up** (`the stand topped up to five, at most twenty tickets a day`). The stand holds none: the assistant prepares a ticket, waits for it ready, leaves it signed (`takeable_until` 24 hours after it asked for it), and so on to five; its next look (every five minutes) finds five and leaves none. The twenty-first ticket of a day is not prepared: the stand stays as it is until the next day (Europe/Rome).
4. **The handshake** (`Bea opens the link: the bond kept at a distance, messaging and ping pong offered to it`). Bea's phone opens the assistant's ticket (BND-10): her introduction reaches the ticket's mailbox; the assistant's collector takes it, the bond `B-BEA` is kept, at a distance, named as she introduced herself; messaging and ping pong are offered to it (EXT-7).
5. **The welcome, held until Messaggi runs on her phone** (`the welcome meets Bea’s silence until she activates Messaggi, then it is delivered`). The bond kept, the assistant writes **the welcome** on it (AST-5), once. Bea has not activated Messaggi: her node is silent (F8), the welcome waits on the relay and at the assistant's post (MSG-2: seven days). She activates it: her collector hands again what the relay keeps (MSG-16), the welcome is received, the assistant's message reads delivered. Seven days without: it reads «nessuna ricevuta», and nothing else is sent.
6. **A question answered** (`Bea asks how the handshake works: one turn, one reply, the chat read up to her message`). Bea writes «Come funziona la stretta di mano?»: the assistant's chat with her has one message to read; its turn covers it; the answerer replies from the copy; the reply is kept in the register, written in the chat, and the chat is marked read up to her message; the turn is closed, its texts dropped from the register.
7. **Three messages, one reply; a fourth, a turn of its own** (`messages before a turn starts are one turn; one that comes while it is answered waits for the next`). Bea writes three messages before the assistant looks: one turn covers the three. A fourth comes while the answerer works: the reply is written after it in the chat, but the chat is marked read only up to the third, so the fourth stays to read and is the next turn.
8. **A request saved as an anonymous note** (`Bea asks to save an idea: a note in richieste/, anonymous and rephrased, and the reply says so`). Bea writes «Sarebbe bello fissare un messaggio in cima alla chat. Salvala come idea». The answerer replies and gives a note; the node writes `richieste/2026-11-28-<words>-<six hex>.md` with the vault's frontmatter, the kind «idea», the request in the answerer's words, its context in Cumino; no name, no bond, no time but the day, no sentence of hers. The reply says it was saved, anonymous.
9. **An idea not asked to be saved** (`an idea told without asking: no note; the reply may offer to save it`). Bea tells an idea without asking to save it: no note; the reply may ask whether she wants it saved.
10. **A question off topic** (`a question off topic: the reply says it answers only on Cumino; the turn counts`). Bea asks for a school essay: the reply says, in a sentence, that it answers only on Cumino. The turn counts in the caps.
11. **Personal data asked for** (`asked about the project’s people: it tells nothing of them`). Bea asks for the name of a relative of the project's people, or for the laptop's paths: the reply tells nothing of them (the copy holds none, AST-11; the system prompt forbids it, AST-10).
12. **The cap of a bond** (`the sixteenth turn of a day on a bond: the notice once, then nothing until tomorrow`). Bea's fifteen turns of the day are closed: her next turn gets **the notice of the cap**, once that day; her later messages are marked read with no reply until the next day.
13. **The cap of the day** (`the sixty-first turn of a day: the notice once per bond, then nothing until tomorrow`). Sixty turns closed in a day, on every bond: each bond's next turn gets the notice of the day, once; nothing more is asked of the answerer until the next day.
14. **One at a time, the oldest first** (`two bonds waiting: one turn at a time, the oldest message first`). Bea's and another visitor's turns wait together: one answerer runs at a time, and the turn whose first message to read is older goes first.
15. **The answerer too slow** (`the answerer over three minutes: stopped, asked again once, then the apology`). The answerer does not answer in 180 seconds: it is stopped (its process group ended), the turn is asked again once; a second time over, **the apology** is written and the turn closed.
16. **The answer out of its form** (`an answer that does not parse, or a reply too long for a message: asked again once, then the apology`). Claude's output does not parse with the answer's schema, or the reply is over 4 000 UTF-16 code units (MSG-4): as story 15.
17. **The answerer not logged in** (`before the bot’s login: no reply, nothing lost; after it, the turns waiting answered in order`). Alessandro has not logged in the bot's configuration yet: the answerer answers `not_logged_in`, no attempt is counted, the turns wait, and the node tries one turn again every ten minutes; once he has logged in, the turns waiting are answered, the oldest first. Their messages read delivered meanwhile (the receipt is the node's, MSG-3), with no reply.
18. **The laptop off, then on** (`stopped at night, started in the morning: what came meanwhile answered once`). The assistant stops at 23:00; Bea writes at 23:30 (her message stays «in uscita», kept by the relay and sent again by her post, MSG-2, PST-6); at 08:00 the assistant starts: its collector takes the message, her message reads delivered, and its turn is answered.
19. **A fall between the reply and the chat** (`stopped after the reply was kept and before it was written: written at the start, once`). The node stops after the register kept the reply and before `write_a_message`: at the start the reply is written under its operation (one message in the chat, DOM-11), then the chat is marked read.
20. **A fall between the chat and the mark** (`stopped after the reply was written and before the chat was marked read: marked at the start, no second reply`). The node stops after the write and before `read_a_chat`: at the start the register's turn, written, is marked read; nothing is asked again.
21. **The replay of every start answers nothing twice** (`a start hands again what the relays keep: no message received twice, no turn`). At each start the collector hands again every letter the relays keep (K-6): each message answered already meets its duplicate branch (DOM-11), no message is received twice, no chat has more to read, and nothing is asked of the answerer.
22. **A ping** (`Bea pings the assistant: pong`). Bea's ping on `B-ASSISTENTE` is answered with a pong (ping pong active and offered, PNG-3); no answerer, no cap.

### The cage and the copy (B3, on the laptop)

23. **The cage refuses a path** (`inside the cage: the copy and /usr, no home, no ssh, no aws, no vault`). In the cage the answerer runs in (a shell in its place, for the test): `/srv/cumino` holds the copy, read-only; `/home/alec`, `~/.ssh`, `~/.aws`, `~/my-brain`, `~/.claude` do not exist; nothing outside `/tmp` and the bot's configuration can be written; the environment holds no variable of Alessandro's. 25. **The copy leaves a file out** (`an allowed note with a word never copied: left out, its path told once`). A note of the allow-list gains a line with a word of the lists (AST-11): the copy leaves the file out and the journal tells its path, never the line. A note not on the allow-list is never copied, whatever it holds.

### End to end (B5)

## For A

The choices of this design, each in the rule that holds it. **High impact** (`[Q, C, high impact: A to discuss]`):

- **A package of this repository, not a separate project** (AST-1; `plan.md` said "a separate project that imports `domains`"): it needs the phone's composition root (`memory`), the extensions' configurators, the relay's HTTP client and B2's storage, none published; here the same CI and the same rules (cruiser, lint) keep it. Rejected: a repository of its own (unpublished packages vendored or published for one consumer; the kernel's changes would break it unseen).
- **The copy is an allow-list, not a list of exclusions** (AST-11): sixteen notes of `projects/q/` are copied, every other file is left out, and a new note enters only when it is added to the list; each file copied is also checked against words never copied. The survey of 27 November found the family and the test community named in about ten notes (among them `scenari`, `sfondo`, `fase-3`, the `import/` transcript and drafts), infrastructure and quota in `notes/`, and session internals in `log.md`. Rejected: a list of exclusions (a new note with a personal line would be copied by default); a redaction by lines (fragile, and the notes are not rewritten, the project's rule).
- **The stand on the relay is a domain of q-'s tree** (INV-1): what the relay keeps is semantics a visitor observes (D114, MBX-2's reason), so it has its office, its facts and its waits, on the oracle and on SQLite. Rejected: a table of the relay's adapter with no domain (a rule only in the code); the page served by Caddy with a script taking the link (INV-7).
- **The inviter signs with a key of the stand's own, not the face's** (INV-5, AST-13). Rejected: the face's key (the configurator would reach into the safe for a signature no persona asks, and one key would serve two credentials).
- **The register outside the kernel's storage, with no tree** (AST-3). Rejected: a tree of the generator for a user's bookkeeping; the unread mark alone (a fall between the reply and its writing would ask Claude twice, and the caps would be counted over every chat).
- **The model `sonnet`, effort `low`** (AST-9), both settings; and the caps' numbers (AST-6): fifteen turns per bond and sixty in all per day, 180 seconds per answer, two attempts.
- **The container as the cage** (part B3, AST-8; Alessandro's choice of Docker): in the container the answerer's process sees what is mounted (the node's storage with the face's private key, the register, the stand's key, the source read-only); only its tools are kept from them (`--restricted`, the deny rules; checked with the real `claude`). Rejected: `bwrap` inside the container; a second container driven through Docker's socket.
- **The copy from the project's worktree** (part B3, AST-11), not master: current notes; the allow-list and the read-only mount keep its work in progress and `richieste/` out.

**Provisional** (`[Q, C, provisional: A to confirm]`): every Italian word (§ Its words), the scripted answerer's test reply among them; the note's frontmatter, `source: derived` (the plan said `source: assistente`, outside the vault schema's values); the face's name; the page's path `/assistente`; «il computer di Alessandro» on the page and in the welcome (the name, public in the repository; or «di chi cura Cumino»).

## Open

- **Abuse of a bond**: a bond over its cap every day is answered every day; withdrawing messaging's offer from it is the user's gesture (EXT-7), which the assistant does not make by itself.
- **The stand emptied on purpose**: anyone may take all the links of a day (INV-9); a limit by address would be Caddy's, with a module it has not, or the relay's, which keeps no address (CFG-3).
- **The copy from master**: the assistant knows the project's notes as master has them (integrated at milestones), behind the project's worktree; the repository's documents are current.
- **The notes' committing**: they are written untracked in the project's worktree and committed at the project's passes; their index, links and tags are the vault's curation.
- **A release of the assistant**: it runs `acceptance`'s commit; when `main` and the release site come, the stand's page points to the release site by configuration (INV-6), the assistant's tickets name the same relay.
- **The bot's login expiring**: Claude Code refreshes its own token in the bot's configuration; if it cannot, story 17's path holds (nothing lost), and the journal says it once.
