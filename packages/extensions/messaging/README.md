# Messaging: `messaging`

The first extension after ping pong (EXT-1; `plan.md`, "After stage 1"): the **chats** of the bonds, written and received through the boundary, preinstalled and off until the user activates it. A bond has its chat: it is not created, it is the bond's. Anna writes to Bruno on their bond: her node records the message she wrote ("I wrote M", F4) with its request on the bond; Bruno's node takes it ("I received M from X", with its annotations) and answers in one event, the **receipt**: his response is what makes her message **delivered** (_consegnato_). Its rules are MSG-1..MSG-16 of [`architecture.md`](../../../docs/architecture.md) (§ 4, "Messaging"), with step 12's (F7, F8, EXT-1..EXT-16, GR-10); its words in the [glossary](../../../docs/glossary.md) ("The words of messaging").

**An MVP** [Q, C, 26 Nov 2026]: designed in part M1 of the night of 26–27 November 2026 (Alessandro, 26 Nov 2026: a long autonomous session on the messaging extension, merged into `acceptance` and published on the test site, preinstalled and off; at 22:40 the goal set to an **MVP** that the test community, eight adult friends on Android with the PWA, can use day to day on their bonds, quality before speed), built in parts M2–M7 (§ Parts M2–M7 below). What the MVP holds: write and read the chats reliably; delivered and not delivered; a peer offline, and the delivery later; what is to read, seen from the home, her contacts (the slot on a contact's row: the last message, how many to read); the history kept across reopenings; a message not delivered, or held, told in its chat and written again from there; the Italian words; the accessibility; the gallery. The model as small as the MVP allows; what it sets aside stays as open scenarios (§ Open), never as dead ends. The Italian words of its screens are provisional (`[Q, C, provisional: A to confirm]`).

Out of it: groups (the kernel's, after), the news channel, the assistant, read receipts (A, 4 Nov 2026: «Niente "letto"»), attachments, editing or deleting a message, push notifications (the PWA has none: a message arrives while the app is open and collects, every five seconds, `flussi` § 4).

## Its tree

The package `packages/extensions/messaging` (`@q-mino/messaging`, EXT-9), on ping pong's model:

- [`business/chats/`](business/chats/), **the chats** (_le chat_), the office that keeps the chats of the bonds: the messages written there, each with where it stands and its instants; the messages received there, each with its annotations; how far she has read each chat. Its components (MSG-1..MSG-7):
  - `write_a_message` (`{ bond, text }`) → `a_message_has_been_written` (the message named by the operation that writes it, `identity_of`, D75.8; the bond, the text, the instant written) with the entry **`deliver_a_message`** (`order: 'any'`, keyed by the message; its payload the message, the bond, the text and `waits`, seven days, `dsl.a_message_waits`), whose return **`close_a_message`** takes `{ bond, message_id, what_came_back }` (the office finds a message in its chat's folder: each closing names the chat with the message, M2), the office's closed set `the_message_was_received`, `the_message_had_no_receipt`, `the_peer_cannot_receive_it`, `the_message_was_called_off`, and records one fact per happening: `a_message_has_been_delivered`, `a_message_has_had_no_receipt`, `a_message_could_not_be_delivered`, `a_message_has_been_called_off`, each with the chat, the message and the instant her node learned it. A message that waits no more is refused, a dead letter.
  - `receive_a_message` (`{ bond, text, declared_at }`, the served side) → `a_message_has_been_received` (the message named by the operation, which on `known` is the request's identity, F7: a request sent again is the same message; the bond, the text, the time the letter declares, the instant her node received it).
  - `read_a_chat` (`{ bond, up_to }`) → `a_chat_has_been_read` (the chat read up to a received message); the same again, or up to one before, `a_chat_has_been_read_again`.
  - The read **`a_chat`** (by bond): its messages, written and received, newest first, whole, then each change as it comes (a stream by message, `stream: { identity: 'message_id' }`), each written message with **its stage** (MSG-13: on its way, delivered, no receipt, could not be delivered, called off, held, put aside), each received one with the time its letter declares (its annotations, MSG-6). The read **`a_chat_at_a_glance`** (by bond), for the row of her contacts: the chat's last message and `to_read`, how many of theirs were filed after the last one she read, then each change (a stream by bond); none before the chat's first message. Both streams composed by the library from the read and the fact log (LIB-8). [Q, C, 26 Nov 2026] (M2) M1 had `a_chat` in pages with the calculations `to_read` and `the_last_message`: a leaf that streams reads whole (LIB-3), and a calculation is a value of each candidate, not of the chat (MSG-7).
- [`persona/self/`](persona/self/), the local user's leaves, below the kernel's node on `self`, which gives the root's context alone (EXT-11): `write_a_message` (`{ bond, text }`, answered `the_message_is_written` with the message; `the_message_cannot_be_written`, the rejection no branch reaches); `read_a_chat` (`{ bond, up_to }`, `the_chat_is_read`); `a_chat` and `a_chat_at_a_glance` (the reads, each its creator `{ query }`, streamed: their surface is the library's since M2, EXT-10).
- [`persona/known/`](persona/known/), the leaf a bonded face invokes, below the kernel's node on `known`, which gives the bond and, as the manifest declares it, the time the request declares (MSG-8): **`message`** (`{ text }`, no refinement, EXT-4), its creator the bond and the declared time from the context, invokes `receive_a_message`; it answers in one event (F7, EXT-4), **`received`**, the receipt; `not_received`, the rejection no branch reaches.
- [`manifest.ts`](manifest.ts), its declaration (MSG-10): the name `messaging`; it sees on `known` the time the request declares (`sees: { known: ['the_declared_time'] }`), not the peer's key nor the bond's name (the bond names the chat; the kernel's screen around the slot names the contact); it touches the port of requests and its storage; two slots, `the_chat` on `a_bond` and `the_chat_in_my_contacts` on `a_bond_in_my_contacts` (MSG-7, MSG-15), the same part (`shows: 'parts/the_chat.js'`; `the_chat` since M2, `the_chat_in_my_contacts` since M4); no action, no page. [`the_manifest.ts`](the_manifest.ts) derives it as data, [`manifest.json`](manifest.json) written by `pnpm --filter @q-mino/messaging manifest`, kept equal by [`test/manifest.test.ts`](test/manifest.test.ts).
- [`texts/it.ts`](texts/it.ts), its words (EXT-16, WEB-22: its name «Messaggi», the slot «Chat»; no action, so no words of events: the part tells its messages in its own, M5), [`texts/it.json`](texts/it.json) written by `pnpm --filter @q-mino/messaging words`, kept equal by [`test/words.test.ts`](test/words.test.ts); [`adapter/delivery.ts`](adapter/delivery.ts), the delivery of its request (MSG-11); [`adapter/partition.ts`](adapter/partition.ts), its state adapter over its partition (EXT-14), a copy of ping pong's with the reads (each over the ranges its selection narrows it to: a chat's folder for a bond) and the traces of the letters discarded (MSG-13); [`adapter/chats.ts`](adapter/chats.ts), the office's records there, a chat's folder under its bond (`state/chat/<bond>/folder`, `state/chat/<bond>/message/<id>`, each identity as `encodeURIComponent` writes it), so that a chat reads as one range, its fragments, its facts' writes and its reads' sources; [`parts/the_chat.tsx`](parts/the_chat.tsx), its part (MSG-7, K-3, part M5), with what it imports of its own: `the_line.tsx` (in its place), `the_conversation.tsx` (on the whole screen), `what_it_reads.ts` (its reads' events parsed by its own surface, how each read stands after each, its follows and invocations through the door), `in_words.ts` and `the_phone.ts` (its times in the phone's zone, its network), `glyphs.tsx`, `its_look.ts`; its words `the_words_of_the_chat` in `texts/it.ts`; [`configurator.ts`](configurator.ts), its configurator and its side of the boundary's messages (EXT-2, EXT-15).

## Its extension points

One part, **the chat** (`parts/the_chat.tsx`), placed by two slots (EXT-16, WEB-30, MSG-7):

- **`the_chat_in_my_contacts`, on `a_bond_in_my_contacts`** (a place new with messaging, MSG-15, K-13): on the row of a contact in her contacts, the home (UI-5), told `{ bond }`. In its place, the chat in a line: the last message, mine or theirs (its first words, and for mine its stage), and how many are to read («2 da leggere»), so that what is new is seen from the home. At her tap it takes the whole screen (WEB-30) and is the conversation.
- **`the_chat`, on `a_bond`**: on the screen of a contact, in messaging's mark beside the other extensions'; in its place the same line; on the whole screen the conversation.
- **The conversation**, the part on the whole screen: the messages of the chat, newest at the bottom, the older ones read as she goes up (pages, MSG-7), each mine with its stage («in uscita», «consegnato», «nessuna ricevuta in 7 giorni», «non consegnabile», «annullato», «trattenuto», «messo da parte») and, when it was not delivered or was called off, the gesture «Invia di nuovo» (MSG-13); each theirs with its annotations at a tap (MSG-6); the field «Scrivi un messaggio» and «Invia». It follows `a_chat` while it is shown, writes (`write_a_message`) and marks the chat read up to the last message it showed (`read_a_chat`): a part acting with no tap of hers (EXT-16), a declared feature, shown as messaging's activity in its mark.
- **No action**: an action is data the kernel draws, which invokes a leaf with the place's data alone (`{ bond }`): it cannot carry a text, and the way into the chat is the slot's own gesture. Rejected: an action «Scrivi» invoking a leaf that writes nothing.
- **No page**: a page is a screen of the extension's own (`on: 'the_extension'`, told `{}`); the list of the chats is the home itself with the slot on each row (UI-5: the contacts stay a view of their own, beside the chats, A, 22 Nov 2026), so the MVP has no page of its own (§ Open).

## Stories

The names: Anna's node, Bruno's and Carla's; Anna's bonds `B-BRUNO` and `B-CARLA`, Bruno's with Anna `B-ANNA`, Carla's with Anna `B-ANNA`; Thursday 26 November 2026, 10:00 in Rome. Messages: Anna's first to Bruno `M1`, «Ciao Bruno», written at 10:00; the request that carries it `R1` (the kernel composes its identity from messaging's name, the effect and `M1`, F7). Each story is the test named after it, in the part that proves it (§ Parts M2–M7).

### Through the boundary (M2)

On a fake kernel that hosts the extension through the boundary's messages, each copied as JSON (as ping pong's `test/kernel.ts`): its storage a partition held as a map; its port of requests answering as the story says; the time that passes ended by the story.

1. **Written and delivered** (`written and delivered: the receipt is Bruno’s response`). Anna writes «Ciao Bruno» in her chat with Bruno: `write_a_message` on `self` with `B-BRUNO` and the text gives `the_message_is_written` with `M1`, and `a_message_has_been_written`. Started, the executor delivers `deliver_a_message`: the port of requests is called with the bond, `usecase/message` on `known` at 1.0.0, `{ text: 'Ciao Bruno' }`, the effect's path, `M1` as the entry's id, 604 800 seconds. Bruno's node answers `received`: `M1` is closed with `a_message_has_been_delivered` at the instant her node learned it, and her chat reads it «consegnato».
2. **Bruno offline for hours, delivered later** (`Bruno’s phone off for eight hours: the message waits on its way, delivered when his app collects it`). Bruno's phone is off from 10:00 to 18:00. `M1` stays on its way («in uscita»): the port's call lasts (F7, PST-6: R1 deposited at 10:00, kept by the relay seven days, sent again at each quarter of its time). Anna closes her app at 12:00. At 18:00 Bruno opens his: his collector takes R1, his node answers `received`. Anna opens hers at 20:00: her collector takes the response, her executor meets R1 again by its identity (EXT-15), and the port answers `received`: `a_message_has_been_delivered` at 20:00, her node's instant, not Bruno's (the receipt says when her node learned it; when his received it is his record).
3. **No receipt within its wait** (`no receipt in seven days: the message closed as such, and written again`). Bruno's phone stays off past 3 December 2026, 10:00: the port answers `no_response`, `M1` closes with `a_message_has_had_no_receipt`, and her chat reads «nessuna ricevuta in 7 giorni» with «Invia di nuovo», which writes `M3`, the same text, a message of its own (MSG-13). A flaw, declared (F6, the principle of the flaws): "no receipt" never means "not received" (MSG-12): a response lost at the last sending leaves Bruno with the message and Anna with no receipt, and «Invia di nuovo» gives him the text twice.
4. **Silence: a node without messaging, or that does not offer it** (`Carla without messaging, or not offering it to Anna: no receipt, the same from outside`). Carla's node has no messaging, or Carla withdrew its offer from her bond with Anna: Carla's node stays silent (F8), her kernel traces the silence with its case (her «Cosa è successo»), and Anna's `M2` closes as story 3 tells, the two cases alike from outside. No receipt is no answer, not a refusal.
5. **Called off** (`called off by Anna’s own withdrawal or deactivation: closed as called off, no dead letter`). Anna withdraws messaging from her bond with Carla while `M2` waits, or deactivates it: her kernel calls R2 off (`not_offered_to_the_bond`, `not_active`), and `M2` closes with `a_message_has_been_called_off`, «annullato»: her own gesture, a meaning (EXT-12), never a dead letter.
6. **Received, with its annotations** (`Bruno receives M1 with what his node can tell of it`). R1 reaches Bruno's `message` on `known` with `{ text: 'Ciao Bruno' }`, below his kernel's node, which gives `B-ANNA` and the time R1 declares, 10:00:00.120: `receive_a_message` records `a_message_has_been_received` with the bond, the text, the declared time and the instant his node received it, 18:00:04 by his clock; the leaf answers `received` in one event. His chat shows «Ciao Bruno» with its annotations (MSG-6): **signature valid** and **known bond** (only a face whose signature holds, at the mailbox of a bond kept with it, reaches `known`: the persona is the annotation, PER-7), **arrived at the mailbox of the bond** (one per bond, BND-11: the bond tells it), **declared at 10:00** (a claim of Anna's node, F2), **received at 18:00** (his node's measure, trusted, F2). **The relay's time is missing**: the relay tells when it kept each deposit, and the kernel drops it on the way (K-5); the annotations say so («ora del relay: non disponibile»).
7. **The same request again, one message** (`R1 sent again: one message in Bruno’s chat, one receipt`). Bruno's response to R1 is lost on its way; Anna's post sends R1 again at the next quarter (PST-6). Bruno's leaf meets the same operation: the duplicate branch gives the recorded fact (DOM-11), `received` again, one `a_message_has_been_received`, one message in his chat, and Anna's `M1` delivered once.
8. **The order of a chat** (`a chat in the order her node recorded it, the declared time beside`). Bruno, offline, writes «arrivo» at 09:30; his phone sends it at 10:15, back online. Anna wrote «dove sei?» at 10:00. Her chat reads «dove sei?» (hers, 10:00), then «arrivo» (received 10:15, declared 09:30): **the order of her node's records**, one clock, hers (MSG-5), the declared time an annotation beside the message. A peer that declares 1 January 2020 does not move its message up her history.
9. **Held, told in the chat, written again** (`a receipt that does not parse: held, told on the message; put aside; written again`). Bruno's node answers R1 with an event that is no event of `message` (a broken or tampered node: the code is free): Anna's delivery is exhausted with his event kept, a dead letter (EXT-2, USR-8), holding nothing behind it (`order: 'any'`: her message to Carla goes on). Her chat reads `M1` «trattenuto» (MSG-13, the stage the read takes from the executor's held entry), with «Invia di nuovo»; the kernel shows the held letter in messaging's mark and in «Cosa è successo», where she may send it again (the port gives the answer it keeps for R1, F7: held again) or put it aside: `M1` reads «messo da parte». «Invia di nuovo» writes `M4`, the same text, a request of its own. The bond removed while `M1` waits: `the_bond_is_gone`, held too, and the chat is no longer on her screens.
10. **Reading a chat as a stream** (`the chat followed: its snapshot, then each change`). The part follows `a_chat` on `B-BRUNO`: first its messages, newest first, whole, then each change as it comes (a message written, delivered, received), until the part is no longer shown (K-4); a message held or put aside is told at the chat's next fact or its next opening (M2's gap G-2). `to_read` is the glance's (story 11).
11. **What is to read, from her contacts** (`the row of Bruno’s contact: the last message and two to read; read in the conversation, none`). Bruno writes twice while Anna's app is open on her contacts: the row of Bruno reads his last message and «2 da leggere» (`the_last_message`, `to_read`). She opens the conversation from the row: both shown, `read_a_chat` up to the second, and the row reads none to read.
12. **The history across reopenings** (`a new configurator over the same partition reads the chat whole, and delivers what was on its way once`). Anna writes `M1` and her app closes before the executor runs; a new configurator over the same partition reads the chat as it was (her earlier messages with their stages, Bruno's with their annotations, how far she had read) and, started, delivers `M1` once.
13. **Ready** (`ready carries messaging, the package’s version and the boundary’s`).

## Kernel gaps

Checked in the code on 26 November 2026 (M1), against what the stories need.

- **A request that waits days: holds.** The boundary's call takes `waits` from 1 to 604 800 seconds (`boundary/src/ports.ts`), `post`'s `waiting_time` the same (PST-5); the post sends again at each quarter (PST-6), each sending a deposit of its own; the executor's waits are durable on the phone (IndexedDB) and fire at the reopening; a timer of seven days stays below `setTimeout`'s limit. A `waits` over the boundary's maximum would not parse at the kernel, and the extension's call would never settle: messaging's `dsl.a_message_waits` is the maximum, typed by its value.
- **The response after the asker's app was closed and reopened: holds.** At each start the collector reads the relay's whole snapshot of each mailbox (it keeps what it saw only while the node runs), so a response kept while the app was closed is collected at the first pass; the executor meets the request again by its identity (EXT-15) and the port follows the post's record until the request closes, with no time of its own.
- **K-1, the served leaf when the peer's app opens: fixed in M1** (MSG-9, [Q, C, high impact: A to discuss]). At the start the collector and the extensions' loading ran side by side: a request collected before messaging ran was answered by silence, traced, and seen; the asker would send it again only at its next quarter, almost two days later for a message. Now the hosting throws `TheExtensionIsNotRunningYet` for a peer's request to an extension active but not running yet (not loaded at the node's start, or loading), for at most `the_hold_of_a_request` (30 seconds) from the hosting's creation or its load's beginning; the kernel's entry throws on, so that the collector leaves the letter unseen and hands it again at its next pass (5 seconds). Tests: `packages/adapters-common/test/extensions/hosting.test.ts`, "a peer’s request before it runs"; story 17 on the oracle, M3.
- **K-2, the declared time: fixed in M1** (MSG-8, [Q, C, high impact: A to discuss]). The kernel's node gave every leaf `declared_at` (PER-23), and the hosting dropped it. Now `sees.known` may name `the_declared_time` (`boundary.the_declared_time`, `context.declared_at`), given by the wiring's `the_declared_time_on_known`, told the user at activation («Vedere l’ora che i tuoi contatti dichiarano nelle loro lettere», provisional).
- **K-3, a part of an extension's, drawn: fixed in M4** (large; [Q, C, high impact: A to discuss]; EXT-16, WEB-23, GR-10 "The parts", as built; M4's notes in § Parts). Its design as M1 proposed it: The boundary declares slots and pages (`manifest.ts`: `{ on, shows }`, `shows` a file left from the frames), the screens draw each slot as the app's fault (`APartNotDrawn`, `adapters-web/src/screens/parts.tsx`), and EXT-16 defers "a component of the extension's in the kernel's frame" until an extension the app ships has one: messaging is that one. Proposed, for M4: a part is a **Preact component** exported by the extension's `parts/<name>.tsx` (outside its tree, as `adapter/`), `shows` naming that module (`parts/the_chat.js`, the emitted path under `/q-/ext/messaging/`); its props: the place's data (`{ bond }`), where the slot is (`WhereASlotIs`), and **the part's door to the kernel**, `invoke(use_case, version, input)` and `follow(use_case, version, input)`, each an `AsyncIterable` of events, sent as `a_message_from_a_part` through `through_a_parts_fence` and `her.a_part_asks` (WEB-23's fence and door, kept for it), a follow ended when the part is no longer shown (K-4). The phone imports the part's module with the extension's realm when it is active (`AnExtensionCarried` gains its parts, `phone/src/node/the_start.ts`, `main.tsx`), and `ThePoints` draws it in place of `APartNotDrawn`. GR-10 opens for an extension's `parts/` to `preact` and `@preact/signals` (the extension's `package.json`, a `tsconfig` of its parts with `jsx` and the DOM, the ESLint rules of Preact extended to `packages/extensions/*/parts`, the cruiser rule `extension-<name>-imports` allowing those two packages); the kit of the kernel's UI pieces stays out: a part draws with plain elements and the design's CSS variables (`--extension-*`, the type scale, the spacing), so that it inherits the theme. Rejected: the conversation drawn by the kernel from messaging's data (EXT-16's discarded vocabulary of the kernel); the frame of 21–24 November (EXT-13's future mode); a kit of the kernel's pieces now (a package and a name for one extension's needs: with its second case).
- **K-4, ending a follow: fixed in M3** (small; [Q, C, high impact: A to discuss]; EXT-2 "Out", "An invocation ended by the kernel"). The boundary had no message that ends an invocation in flight: a part's follow of a live read (`a_chat`) would run in the extension until it unloads, one more each time the slot is shown. Now `{ message: 'end', id }` in `a_message_to_an_extension`; the extension's side sends no more of that flow's events, asks `return()` of the leaf's iterator, and says `done`; the hosting sends it from its flow's `finally` when its consumer stops before `done` (a part no longer shown; a peer's request answered by its first event too), and drops what still crosses, its `done` included. A limit, declared: the leaves and the library's mounted port are async generators, so a consumer that stops while waiting is heard at the follow's next event, and the extension's composed stream of an abandoned follow stops at its next event (each fact reads it again until then, G-3); an end heard while waiting comes with its case. Tests: `packages/boundary/test/messages.test.ts`, `adapters-common/test/extensions/hosting.test.ts` ("an invocation ended by its consumer"), story 16 on the oracle (the follow ended once it read the message delivered).
- **K-5, the relay's time: after the MVP.** The relay's collect gives `deposited_at` per deposit (`desk/holder/usecase.ts`), dropped by the phone's relay client (`a_snapshot`), the collector (`ADeposit`), the entry (`AnEnvelopeCollected`) and the persona's context. Proposed: carried through those to the persona base's `takes` (`kept_by_the_relay_at`, the relay's claim, per deposit: each sending again has its own), then `sees: { known: ['the_relay_time'] }` as K-2. The MVP tells it as missing (story 6): an annotation of the relay's, not what the chat needs to work.
- **K-6, the collector's position is not durable: after the MVP.** Every start replays what each relay keeps (up to seven days): harmless (each letter meets its duplicate branch, each response the relay's), and what K-12 relies on, but a node with long chats invokes messaging once per message kept at every start.
