# Ping pong: `ping_pong`

The first extension (EXT-1; step 14, part 14.1): a feature that is not the kernel's, wired through the boundary, preinstalled and off until the user activates it. Anna pings her bond with Bruno; Bruno's node answers with a pong; Anna's node records the pong, that the ping had no response within its time, or that Bruno's node could not answer it, and her screen follows the ping to it. Its rules are PNG-1..PNG-6 of [`architecture.md`](../../../docs/architecture.md), with step 12's (F7, F8, EXT-1..EXT-16, GR-10) and DOM-3 (a leaf's wait); its words in the [glossary](../../../docs/glossary.md) ("The words of ping pong").

- Its tree (`sight.ts`, generated by `pnpm generate`): [`business/pings/`](business/pings/), the office that pings the bonds and keeps what came back (`send_a_ping`, whose request `ask_for_a_pong` returns to `close_a_ping`; `answer_a_ping` on the served side); [`persona/self/`](persona/self/) (`send_a_ping`, the action on a bond, which follows the ping to its closing) and [`persona/known/`](persona/known/) (`ping`, answered in one event: the pong), mounted below the kernel's nodes (EXT-11).
- [`manifest.ts`](manifest.ts), its declaration; [`the_manifest.ts`](the_manifest.ts), the manifest as data, derived; [`manifest.json`](manifest.json), written by `pnpm --filter @q-mino/ping_pong manifest` ([`scripts/manifest.ts`](scripts/manifest.ts)), kept equal to the derivation by [`test/manifest.test.ts`](test/manifest.test.ts) (EXT-10).
- [`adapter/delivery.ts`](adapter/delivery.ts): the delivery of its request (EXT-12, PNG-6).
- Part 14.2: its state adapter over its partition (`adapter/partition.ts`, `adapter/pings.ts`, EXT-14) and its configurator (`configurator.ts`, with the boundary's side, EXT-2, EXT-15; the package's export `.`), on `greetings`' model; the stories below as tests.

## Stories

The names: Anna's node, Bruno's and Carla's, Anna's bonds `B-BRUNO` and `B-CARLA`, Bruno's with Anna `B-ANNA`; 20 November 2026, 10:00 in Rome. Each story is the test named after it.

### Through the boundary

In `test/stories.test.ts`, on a fake kernel that hosts the extension through the boundary's messages, each copied as JSON (as `greetings`' `test/kernel.ts`): its storage a partition held as a map, each commit all or none; its port of requests answering as the story says; the time that passes a real timer, ended by the story.

1. **A ping and its pong** (`a ping and its pong: the pong received, Anna’s screen follows it`). Anna taps "Ping" on her bond with Bruno: `send_a_ping` on `self` with the bond gives `the_ping_is_sent`, the ping named by her operation, and `a_ping_has_been_sent`. Started, the executor delivers `ask_for_a_pong`: the port of requests is called with the bond, `usecase/ping` on `known` at 1.0.0, `{}`, the effect's path, the ping as the entry's id, 60 seconds. Bruno's node answers `pong`: the ping is closed with `a_pong_has_been_received`, and Anna's screen, which followed the ping, reads `pong` and ends.
2. **No response** (`no response within its time: the ping had none, and the screen reads it`). Bruno's phone stays off for the 60 seconds: the port answers `no_response`, the ping is closed with `a_ping_has_had_no_response`, and Anna's screen reads `no_response`.
3. **The peer cannot answer** (`the peer cannot answer: not_found, version_not_supported, invalid_request, no_pong`). Bruno's node closes the request (`not_found`), refuses it (`version_not_supported`, `invalid_request`), or its office gives no pong (`no_pong`): the ping is closed with `a_ping_could_not_be_answered`, and the screen reads `the_peer_cannot_answer`.
4. **Refused, or the bond gone** (`refused, or the bond gone: a dead letter, shown, the ping still waiting`). The port refuses the request (the offer withdrawn from the bond, `not_offered_to_the_bond`) or the bond is gone: the delivery is exhausted with its reason, a dead letter the kernel shows (`dead_letter`, its name the effect's path and the ping); the ping still waits, and its screen's wait ends with `the_wait_is_over`.
5. **A response that does not parse** (`a response that does not parse is a dead letter, and Carla’s pong is not held behind it`). Anna pings Bruno and Carla; Bruno's node answers `greeted_back`, no event of `ping` (the code is free): Bruno's ping is a dead letter with his event kept, and Carla's pong is received, nothing held behind it (`order: 'any'`).
6. **The screen's wait over** (`the screen’s wait is over at 70 seconds and records nothing; the ping closes after`). The port answers only after Anna's screen stopped waiting: the screen reads `the_wait_is_over`, nothing recorded; the ping is closed later with what came back, a fact the history keeps.
7. **Bruno's node answers** (`Bruno’s leaf ping answers pong in one event; the request again, one pong; malformed, invalid`). Anna's request P1 reaches Bruno's `ping` on `known` with the bond: one event, `pong`, and `a_ping_has_been_answered` recorded with the bond. P1 sent again: the same event, one pong (DOM-11). An input with a field: `invalid_request`; a path that is no leaf: `not_a_leaf`.
8. **A stop** (`a ping survives a stop: a new configurator over the same partition delivers it once`). Anna pings Bruno and her node stops before its executor runs; a new configurator over the same partition finds the ping (her tap again is the recorded fact) and, started, delivers it once.
9. **Ready** (`ready carries ping_pong, the package’s version and the boundary’s`). Loaded and wired, the extension says `ready` with `ping_pong`, its package's version and the boundary version it was built with.

### Between two nodes, on q-'s oracle

In `packages/memory/test/extensions/ping_pong.test.ts`, on the node of q-'s oracle (`packages/memory/test/node/harness.ts`, the cast of `annas_phone.ts`), ping pong in place of `greetings`: the step's checks (`plan.md`, step 14).

10. **Preinstalled and off** (`ping pong preinstalled and off: Bruno’s ping meets silence until Anna activates it`). Anna's node has ping pong installed, never activated: Bruno's ping meets silence, Anna's kernel traces `the_extension_is_not_active`, and Bruno's node records `a_ping_has_had_no_response` at its time. Anna activates it, accepting the manifest as shown: Bruno's next ping gets its pong.
11. **A ping and its pong between two nodes** (`a ping between two nodes with ping pong active gives its pong`). Both have activated it: Anna's ping reaches Bruno's `ping` on `known` through his kernel's entry, below his node of `known`, and its pong comes back as the return of her request: `a_pong_has_been_received` on Anna's node, `a_ping_has_been_answered` on Bruno's.
12. **No response from a node without it, or that does not offer it** (`to a node without ping pong, or not offering it to that bond, no response: the same from outside`). Carla's node has no ping pong installed; Bruno withdraws its offer from Anna: Anna's pings to each meet silence and are closed with `a_ping_has_had_no_response`, the two alike from outside, each silence in its node's kernel's trace with its case. Bruno's own ping to Anna, sent after his withdrawal, is refused by his port of requests (`not_offered_to_the_bond`), nothing sent: a dead letter on his node.
13. **A ping redelivered** (`a ping redelivered gives one pong`). Bruno's pong to P1 is lost on its way; Anna's post sends P1 again with the same identity: Bruno's duplicate branch gives the recorded fact, the same pong, one `a_ping_has_been_answered` on his node and one `a_pong_has_been_received` on Anna's.
14. **Two pings in flight** (`Anna pings Bruno (P1, his phone off) and Carla (P2): P1 no response, P2 its pong, none behind the other`). F7's scenario: P2's pong is received while P1 waits; Anna's phone closed and started again, P2 delivered again reads Carla's pong from the port's kept answer, one pong, no dead letter (DOM-14); P1 had no response at its time.
