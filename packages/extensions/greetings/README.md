# The test extension: `greetings` (`saluti`)

The test extension of step 12 (EXT-9; part 12.4): a feature that is not the kernel's, wired through the boundary, small enough to prove the boundary, the manifest, the partition and the request before ping pong (step 14). Anna greets her bond with Bruno; Bruno's node answers with a greeting back; Anna's node records the greeting back, or that it had no response within its time. Its rules are EXT-1..EXT-16 of [`architecture.md`](../../../docs/architecture.md), with F7, F8, GR-10, PER-6 ("A mounted folder") and DOM-13..DOM-15; its words in the [glossary](../../../docs/glossary.md) (`saluti`, the greeting, the greeting back, its words).

- Its tree (`sight.ts`, generated by `pnpm generate`): [`business/greetings/`](business/greetings/), the office that greets the bonds and keeps what came back (`greet_a_bond`, whose request `ask_for_a_greeting_back` returns to `close_a_greeting`; `greet_a_bond_back` on the served side); [`persona/self/`](persona/self/) (`greet_a_bond`, the action on a bond) and [`persona/known/`](persona/known/) (`greet`, answered in one event), mounted below the kernel's nodes (EXT-11).
- [`manifest.ts`](manifest.ts), its declaration; [`the_manifest.ts`](the_manifest.ts), the manifest as data, derived; [`manifest.json`](manifest.json), written by `pnpm --filter @q-mino/greetings manifest` ([`scripts/manifest.ts`](scripts/manifest.ts)), kept equal to the derivation by a test (EXT-10).
- [`adapter/`](adapter/): its state adapter over its partition (`partition.ts`, `greetings.ts`, EXT-14) and the delivery of its request (`delivery.ts`, EXT-12).
- [`configurator.ts`](configurator.ts): its composition root, wired and not started, and `greetings`, the extension over the boundary's side of the messages (EXT-2, EXT-15), what the kernel hosts.

## Stories

Each story is the test named after it, in [`test/stories.test.ts`](test/stories.test.ts), on a fake kernel ([`test/kernel.ts`](test/kernel.ts)) that hosts the extension through the boundary's messages, each copied as JSON: its storage a partition held as a map, each commit all or none; its port of requests answering as the story says. The names: Anna's node and Bruno's, their bond `B-BRUNO` on Anna's side and `B-ANNA` on Bruno's, Carla's bond `B-CARLA`; 20 November 2026, 10:00 in Rome.

1. **A greeting and its greeting back** (`a greeting and its greeting back recorded`). Anna taps "Saluta" on her bond with Bruno: `greet_a_bond` on `self` with the bond gives `the_greeting_is_sent`, the greeting named by her operation. Started, the executor delivers `ask_for_a_greeting_back`: the port of requests is called with the bond, `usecase/greet` on `known` at 1.0.0, `{ words: 'ciao' }`, the effect's path, the greeting as the entry's id, 60 seconds. Bruno's node answers `greeted_back`, "ciao anche a te": the greeting is closed with `the_greeting_came_back` and his words.
2. **No response** (`no response within its time`). Bruno's phone stays off for the 60 seconds: the port answers `no_response`, and the greeting is closed with `the_greeting_had_no_response`.
3. **The peer cannot answer** (`the peer cannot answer: …`). Bruno's node closes the request (`not_found`), refuses it (`version_not_supported`, `invalid_request`), or its office does not greet back (`not_greeted_back`): the greeting is closed with `the_peer_cannot_answer`.
4. **A fault: refused, or the bond gone** (`… : a dead letter, shown`). The port refuses the call (undeclared, `not_declared`; its input over what a letter carries, `too_large_for_a_letter`) or the bond is gone: the delivery is exhausted with its reason, a dead letter the kernel shows (`dead_letter`, its name the effect's path and the greeting); the greeting still waits.
5. **A response that does not parse** (`a response that does not parse is a dead letter, and the effect goes on toward another bond`). Anna greets Bruno and Carla; Bruno's node answers `pong`, no event of `greet` (the code is free): Bruno's greeting is a dead letter with his event kept, and Carla's greeting back is recorded, nothing held behind it (`order: 'any'`).
6. **The user redelivers** (`the user redelivers a dead letter: delivered, let go`). Bruno's greeting is a dead letter, the bond gone; the bond is back, Anna redelivers it from its mark: delivered, the greeting is closed with his greeting back, and the letter is `released`.
7. **Bruno's node greets back** (`Bruno’s leaf greet answers greeted_back in one event; the request again, the same; malformed, invalid`). Anna's request P1 reaches Bruno's `greet` on `known` with the bond: one event, `greeted_back` with "ciao anche a te", and the greeting back recorded with her words. P1 sent again: the same event, one greeting back (DOM-11). An input with no words: `invalid_request`; a path that is no leaf: `not_a_leaf`.
8. **A stop** (`the state survives a stop and a new configurator over the same partition`). Anna greets Bruno and her node stops before its executor runs; a new configurator over the same partition finds the greeting (her gesture again is the recorded fact) and, started, delivers it once.
9. **All or none** (`a commit is all or none: a full partition keeps nothing of the greeting`). The partition is full: Anna's greeting is refused whole, the extension `fallen`, nothing written.
10. **Ready** (`ready carries the manifest’s name, the package’s version and the boundary’s`). Loaded and wired, the extension says `ready` with `greetings`, its package's version and the boundary version it was built with.
11. **Called off** (`called off, …: the greeting closed with it, no dead letter`; A, 21 Nov 2026). The port refuses the request because Anna withdrew greetings from the bond (`not_offered_to_the_bond`) or deactivated it (`not_active`), a new request or one in flight the kernel called off: her own gesture, a meaning, so the greeting is closed with `the_greeting_was_called_off`, and no dead letter (EXT-12).

Beside them: [`test/manifest.test.ts`](test/manifest.test.ts) (the committed `manifest.json` is the derived one; `greet` answers in one event, with the kernel's events; an action whose leaf is no leaf of `self` does not compile) and [`test/mounted.test.ts`](test/mounted.test.ts) (the mounted points, EXT-11).
