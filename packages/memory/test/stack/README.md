# The scenarios of stage 1 on the `memory` stack

Step 17 of the [plan](../../../../docs/plan.md): phones and the relay in one process, `mailbox` imported in place of HTTP and reached only through the personas of the relay's desk (CFG-3, PER-19 of [`architecture.md`](../../../../docs/architecture.md)); the scenarios of stage 1 end to end, every one under injected loss and duplication on the mailbox (the step's Checks).

## The stack

[`src/stack/`](../../src/stack/index.ts):

- `a_stack(relay_cell, release)(world)`: the relay over its cell (a new relay's unless given), started in its world (`clock`, `elapsed`, and the `hash` of its desk's interface, R into D, the one the phones' safes draw D with, SEA-4), its waits' executor wired and not started (`relay.waits()`); `a_phone({ world, cell, release, transports })`, a phone of [`src/phone/`](../../src/phone/index.ts) over its cell (a new phone's unless given), the stack's transports given through the hook `transports`, so that a test decorates them and an adapter's step puts its own in their place. The stack starts no consumer: what runs, and when, is its caller's.
- `the_relay_s_transports(desk, { clock, hash })`: the two transports in process (ADP-4). The post's mailbox port: a deposit shows nothing, `the_desks_interface` gives the `Caller`, and the depositor's `deposit_an_envelope` takes it at the relay's instant, under the post's operation in the depositor's scope (`the_scope_of_a_deposit`, PST-11); the relay's answer told in the post's words (`kept`, `kept_already` for `identity_already_used`, else `refused` with the event, PST-10). The collect port: the mailbox's R shown, the holder's `collect_the_envelopes`, the deposits of its first snapshot, the stream let go; a read that begins otherwise throws, a transport that fails. The post's harness ([`../post/harness.ts`](../post/harness.ts), `a_transport`) and the node's ([`../node/harness.ts`](../node/harness.ts)) carry their deposits and collects through them, their faults decorating them.

## The suite

[`stories.ts`](stories.ts), `stories_of_the_stack(name, factory, plans)`: the scenarios as a suite parametrized by a factory (TST-1), the phones' primitives and their transports from the stack's in process. [`stack.test.ts`](stack.test.ts) runs it on q-'s oracle over the safe's fake primitives under both fault plans, and over libsodium's primitives (SEA-9) under the lossy plan: 21 tests, about 20 seconds. Steps 20 (the relay's HTTP client), 21 (IndexedDB) and 25 (the phone) run the same suite with their factories.

[`harness.ts`](harness.ts), `a_story(factory, plan)`:

- **The phones**: Anna, Bruno, Carla, Dario (phones 1–4), each with no face at its start, ping pong 0.1.0 preinstalled and off (PNG-1), the world's random bytes drawn per phone. A phone is **on**: her app open (`the_app`), the kernel's four executors and the two reactions running, the extensions following the office (EXT-15); or **off** (`off`): all of them stopped, its extensions unloaded, and it collects nothing.
- **The clock**: the phones' and the relay's, at 10:00 UTC on Friday 20 November 2026. Moved (`settles_through_the_quarters`), it ends every wait asked for. **`settles`** is the time a story lets pass with the clock still: rounds of the executors' pauses over (a transient delivery tried again, DOM-14), the post's executor started again after an abort (PST-10: the effect stopped unadvanced, met again when it starts), every phone on collecting once (a collect lost borne, the mailbox left for the next pass), then the event loop quiet; over when two rounds in a row change nothing the ports show (the facts of every cell, the deposits asked for, what each collector handed, the traces, the letters held, the extension's partition and life), at most 60 rounds. A request whose answer was lost is asked again at the next quarter of its time (PST-6): a story settles through the quarters while what it waits for has not come (a ping's or a question's 15 seconds, an introduction's 12 hours).
- **The gestures** are her activities (USR-2), asked with an id the story draws and read back as her screens read them (`the_end_of`): never the address book's commands.
- **What a story observes** (TST-3): the activities, the address book's reads (`my_faces`, `a_bond`, the ticket shown), the safe's key of a face, the office as the kernel reads it, the facts of the log, the post's answers, the kernel's trace, the letters held as she is told them (USR-8), the pongs in ping pong's partition, the deposits each transport was asked for, what each collector handed the entry.

## The faults (TST-4, ADP-5)

Decorators of a phone's transports, in the harness, under a plan: `none`, or `lossy`, a deterministic schedule per phone and per transport.

- **Deposits**: `lost` (never reaches the relay, answered `unreachable`), `answer_lost` (kept, answered `unreachable`), `doubled` (deposited twice under its operation), `aborted` (the transport throws). The lossy cycle: lost, doubled, answer_lost, aborted, doubled. Every deposit meets one, requests and responses alike; a loss is always followed by a duplication, which reaches the relay.
- **Collects**: `lost` (the collect throws, the collector leaves the mailbox for its next pass) and `doubled` (every envelope of the snapshot given twice). The lossy cycle: doubled, lost, doubled, none.

What the stack does with them: a request's deposit lost or its answer lost is transient, tried again after the executor's pause, under the same operation (the relay's duplicate branch, `kept_already`); an abort stops the post's executor's deposits, which go on once it is started again; a response's deposit lost is traced (`a_response_not_deposited`) and found by the asker's sending again (PST-6), the server answering from its recorded fact; a response's deposit aborted makes the entry throw, so the collector hands the request again at its next pass and the leaf meets its operation again (DOM-11); an envelope given twice by a collect is handed once (DOM-17). Under the lossy plan every scenario that touches the mailbox checks that a deposit of each phone that writes in it, and a collect, met a fault since its point of interest (`met_the_faults`); under `none`, that none did.

## Open points and deviations

- **The link** travels as the phone shows the ticket, passed as is: `packages/memory` may import `@q-mino/adapters-web` under the cruiser's rules, but its tests compile without the DOM's lib, which `adapters-web`'s index needs. The ticket through the link's text (`the_link_of_a_ticket`, `the_ticket_in_a_link`) is proven in `adapters-web` (step 23) and comes end to end with the phone (step 25).
- **The opener's confirmation at a distance**: Carla's bond stays `awaited` after Anna's answer to her introduction (`you_are_bonded`): the confirmation from the introduction's response is not written. Her confirmation comes with Anna's next letter on the bond (BND-5).
- **A second first face** ends `done_already`, as the port's link of `self`'s events says it (`you_have_a_face_already`, USR-3).
- **The relay's sight of a collect** (F8, "Another": "the relay sees that the node collected the letter"): a collect is a read of the holder's, which records no fact, so the stories show it by the collector of the phone that collected (Carla's), not at the relay.
- **The first face** touches no mailbox: the lossy plan meets no fault there.
