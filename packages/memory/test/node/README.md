# The node of q-'s oracle

[`harness.ts`](harness.ts): a node of q-'s oracle as the kernel runs it (part 13.3; PER-22, PER-24, PER-25, EXT-6, MBX-7 of [`architecture.md`](../../../../docs/architecture.md)), the ground of the tests of the kernel's entry, of auth and of the personas, and of the two-node checks of step 12 ([`../extensions/harness.ts`](../extensions/harness.ts)).

- **One cell per phone**: the states of `seals` (the safe), `post` (the requests), `bonds` (the address book) and `extensions` (the office), each domain's ports over it (`seen_by`); the reactions of `reaction/` (the first face, the ticket); the executors of the safe, the post (its deposits through a transport over the relay's oracle, where a story injects its faults, `will`), the address book (an introduction it hands to `post` is kept, `letters`, and carried by the story) and the office (a question of the extensions in common delivered for real, `the_delivery_of_the_extensions_in_common` over the kernel's own port of requests, `the_kernels_port_of_requests`: the node's post, its machine, the address book's bond kept or gone; the peer's entry answers it from `the_extensions_in_common` on `known`, EXT-8); the story's clock (`a_story`), moved by the story, every wait then over.
- **Its face**, made at its first start from the app (`self`'s `make_my_first_face`), its keys the safe's through the reactions.
- **Its bonds made for real**, by tickets and introductions through the address book's own commands, as its stories make them (`bonded_by_a_link`: the holder's ticket opened by the other node, its introduction carried back and accepted; `bonded_in_person`: the QR crossed, the second introduction carried or kept back), with the ids the story chooses (`B-BRUNO`, `L_b`), so that keys, ticket secrets and one mailbox per bond are real; a ticket left ready where a story needs one (`prepares_a_ticket`).
- **The collector** (`collects`): every mailbox the node collects (`the_mailboxes_i_collect`), at the relay, with its R from the safe; each deposit not seen yet handed to the entry as `{ came_to, envelope }`; one the entry throws on stays unseen, the mailbox's next deposits behind it (DOM-17), and the pass throws once over. The relay's desk hashes R as the safe draws D (`the_hash_of_a_collect_secret`), so that the safe's mailboxes are the relay's.
- **The kernel's entry** (`the_kernels_entry`), wired: the machine at my mailbox (the face of mine whose bond or ticket has it, none otherwise); the four trees by `zz.tree_ports_by_path` over q-'s persona registries, invoking the address book's and the office's use cases over the cell and auth's lookups by `zz.lookup_ports` over `the_acquaintances` (over the cell's address book and office reads); the routing over the office (`the_kernels_reads`) and auth's `the_extensions_offered_to_the_bond_of_a_key` (names only); the post's `take_a_response` and its mailbox port; the bond to answer on (PER-25, 5: the bond kept at the mailbox the request came to with that key, else a bond kept with that key, else none); the kernel's trace (`traced`) and the trees' (`tree_traced`) kept for the story.
- **The extension its app carries** (`installed`: not installed, `greetings` by default, or `ping_pong`, part 14.2), hosted in process (EXT-13) over its partition, its leaves mounted by `zz.mounted_leaf_ports` on `self` and below q-'s node of `known` (`the_bond_on_known` from the node's `context.bond`); its requests sent through the kernel's port of requests over the cell's post, address book and office. A node's code changed (the code is free, F2): `tampers_with_its_responses`.
- **The user's offices** (part 15.2, USR-1..USR-9): `user`'s three offices in the cell beside the kernel's; `user`'s executor delivering `carry_out_an_activity` through the port toward `self` (`the_delivery_of_an_activity`: the kernel's `self` by path as the user, the extension's `self` through its hosting, the kernel's held letters through the executors' `redeliver` and `discard`) while the app is open (`opens_the_app`, `closes_the_app`); her gestures (`asks`, `keeps_a_draft`, `drops_a_draft`, `changes_face`) and reads (`the_activity`, `how_it_ended`, `a_draft`, `the_active_face`); the news (`held_letters`, `what_happened`). A screen of the extension follows for its time where the story says (`screens_follow: 'for_their_time'`: a wait of a minute or more over once the story's clock has moved past it), at once over otherwise; the partition's next commits as the story scripts them (`its_partition_will`: `refused` makes the extension fall, EXT-15).
- **`self` as the user's entry will invoke it** (step 15): its tree by path, the `Caller` the user (`the_user`, `as_the_user`); the user's gestures (`activates`, `withdraws_the_offer_from`, `removes_a_bond`, `renames`, `greets`, `pings`) go through it.
- **Anna's phone and her acquaintances** ([`annas_phone.ts`](annas_phone.ts)): the cast of the personas' and the entry's stories (Anna; Bruno, the QR crossed, L_b; Carla's link opened, L_c; Anna's ticket L_d ready, Dario's for her), greetings on Anna's phone active, installed or not, her partition's size; the letters they write, real ones through the asker's post (`asks`, the answer its post keeps) or written by the story itself (`writes`).
- **Letters a story writes itself** (`a_request`, `a_response`, `an_envelope_of`: signed by the node's face, unsigned, or with a signature given; `deposits` at any D), for forged, unsigned, re-addressed or malformed letters.

Every wait is bounded and turns on a real timer (`until`, `quiet`).

Rejected: the cells side by side of part 12.4 (a phone of the post's harness beside an office over an address book written as state): two safes, so the face that signs letters and the address book's face would differ, and a ticket's secret is the signature of the address book's face (BND-9).
