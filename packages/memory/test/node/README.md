# The node of q-'s oracle

[`harness.ts`](harness.ts): a node of q-'s oracle as the kernel runs it (parts 13.3 and 17; PER-22, PER-24, PER-25, EXT-6, MBX-7, CFG-1, CFG-2 of [`architecture.md`](../../../../docs/architecture.md)), the ground of the tests of the kernel's entry, of auth and of the personas, of the two-node checks of step 12 ([`../extensions/harness.ts`](../extensions/harness.ts)) and of `user`'s stories.

- **The phone of the `memory` stack** ([`src/phone/`](../../src/phone/index.ts), `phone(cell, release)(world)`): the one wiring of a phone over q-'s oracle, which the harness starts over a cell of its own. It wires the five domains' ports over the cell, the reactions, the executors (the post's deposits, the address book's introductions, the office's question of the extensions in common over the kernel's port of requests, `user`'s activities through the port toward `self`), auth's lookups, the four persona trees by path, the kernel's entry with the bond to answer on, the extension the app carries hosted in process and mounted on `self` and `known`, the collector, the ticket shown, and the consumer that loads the extensions as the office says; the consumers wired, not started.
- **The story's world**: the story's clock (`a_story`), moved by the story, every wait then over; the world's random bytes for this node; the post's deposits through a transport over the relay's oracle, where a story injects its faults (`will`); the relay's collect at the node's mailboxes; the extension its app carries (`installed`: not installed, `greetings` by default, or `ping_pong`).
- **The story's release** (what is the story's, not the phone's): an introduction the address book hands to `post` kept (`letters`) and carried by the story (`carries`, `receives`), so that the stories make their bonds with the ids they choose (`bonded_by_a_link`, `bonded_in_person`, `prepares_a_ticket`); the time in the extension's realm (`screens_follow: 'for_their_time'`: a wait of a minute or more over once the story's clock has moved past it, at once over otherwise); the partition's size and its next commits (`its_partition_will`: `refused` makes the extension fall, EXT-15); a node's code changed (the code is free, F2: `tampers_with_its_responses`); the messages to the extension's realm, watched (`to_the_extension`).
- **The story's affordances**: the node runs until quiet (`runs`: the reactions and the four kernel's executors started, then stopped), its collector passes (`collects`, `serves`); the user's gestures as the user's entry will send them on `self` (`the_user`, `activates`, `deactivates`, `renames`, `greets`, `pings`, …) and from her screens (`asks`, `keeps_a_draft`, `changes_face`, with the app open, `opens_the_app`); her reads and her news (`how_it_ended`, `held_letters`, `what_happened`); the address book's commands as its stories send them; letters a story writes itself (`a_request`, `a_response`, `an_envelope_of`, `deposits`), for forged, unsigned, re-addressed or malformed letters; the extension's partition read.
- **Anna's phone and her acquaintances** ([`annas_phone.ts`](annas_phone.ts)): the cast of the personas' and the entry's stories (Anna; Bruno, the QR crossed, L_b; Carla's link opened, L_c; Anna's ticket L_d ready, Dario's for her), greetings on Anna's phone active, installed or not; the letters they write, real ones through the asker's post (`asks`) or written by the story itself (`writes`).

The introductions delivered for real, as the kernel's requests, are proven with the phone's own delivery in [`../bonds/the_introduction_through_post.test.ts`](../bonds/the_introduction_through_post.test.ts).

Every wait is bounded and turns on a real timer (`until`, `quiet`).

Rejected: the cells side by side of part 12.4 (a phone of the post's harness beside an office over an address book written as state): two safes, so the face that signs letters and the address book's face would differ, and a ticket's secret is the signature of the address book's face (BND-9).
